# Third-Party Services and Attribution

HPP is a dependency-free static application. It does not currently bundle third-party JavaScript libraries, fonts, analytics SDKs, or tracking pixels.

## City of Newton GIS

HPP queries public City of Newton GIS services from the user's browser to resolve a Newton property and retrieve property context.

- City of Newton GIS: https://www.newtonma.gov/government/information-technology/gis
- Newton GIS services: https://gisweb.newtonma.gov/server/rest/services/Data/MapServer

City data remains subject to the City's applicable terms, data limitations, and source information.

## Esri / ArcGIS

The Newton GIS services are ArcGIS services. HPP displays attribution in the website footer and uses the services subject to applicable Esri terms.

- Esri terms: https://www.esri.com/en-us/legal/terms/web-site-service
- Esri attribution guidance: https://developers.arcgis.com/arcgis-rest-js/terms-of-use/

HPP does not bundle or redistribute the ArcGIS software itself.

## Other services

Google Analytics and Vercel Analytics are not currently enabled in the repository. If either is added, the implementation, privacy disclosures, and applicable terms will be reviewed before activation.
