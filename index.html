ABOUT

A clearer way through a complicated process.

A home project rarely fits into one department. A basement renovation can raise building, fire, electrical, plumbing, energy, and egress questions. An addition can also involve zoning, setbacks, lot coverage, trees, conservation, historic review, engineering, and inspections.

Home Project Planner brings those pieces together around one address and one defined project. It starts with official property information, asks only questions that can change the planning path, and turns the result into an ordered set of requirements, documents, applications, inspections, and follow-up work.

The goal is not to replace the City, a designer, contractor, architect, engineer, or other qualified professional. The goal is to make the information you need easier to find, understand, and act on before you spend money or start work.

03
MISSION

Turn scattered local requirements into clear, usable next steps.

For a homeowner, the difficult part of a project is often not knowing that a permit exists. It is figuring out which department matters, which application applies, what documents have to be prepared first, whether another review has to happen before the building permit, and what still has to be inspected or closed out later.

Home Project Planner is built to answer those practical questions. It connects a specific property and project to the City information that can affect it, organizes the process in the order a homeowner actually needs to follow, and links each major step back to the relevant official source.

Where the available information does not establish an answer, the planner should say so and identify what needs to be checked. That is not the end of the process; it is a signal to verify the specific condition with the appropriate City department or professional before relying on it.

The standard is simple: fewer dead ends, fewer disconnected web pages, and a clearer path from “I want to change my house” to “I know what I need to investigate and what I should do next.”
