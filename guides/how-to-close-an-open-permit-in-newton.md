# How do I close an open permit in Newton, MA?

> Old permits do not close automatically. The completed work has to be inspected. Close the electrical, plumbing, gas and other trade permits first, then request the final building inspection. Only the owner or the contractor of record should call.

## What the City says

- Old permits do not close automatically; completed work must be inspected. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))
- Check the complete list of open permits. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))
- Find your district inspector using the GIS browser linked on the City's closure page. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))
- Close electrical, plumbing, gas and other department permits before requesting the final building inspection. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))
- Only the property owner or contractor of record should call. Inspectors will not schedule closure inspections for brokers, agents, or new buyers. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))
- Current work gets scheduling priority over old projects. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))
- Don't pay a project in full until the final inspection and the permit is closed in the system. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))

## Questions and answers

### Do old permits expire or close on their own?

No. The City says they do not close automatically. The completed work must be inspected. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))

### Can my real estate agent schedule the closing inspection?

No. The City says inspectors will not schedule closure inspections for brokers, agents, or new buyers. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))

### In what order do permits close?

Trade permits (electrical, plumbing, gas and others) close before the final building inspection. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))

### When should I pay my contractor in full?

The City advises waiting until after the final inspection and closure in the system. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))
