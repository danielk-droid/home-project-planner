# What permits and process are required to add or upgrade a gas line in Newton, MA?

> Gas piping work in Newton is handled through the ISD gas-permit process. Newton requires appropriate signed license documentation for gas permit applications. A project that involves excavation or a public right-of-way may also require Engineering review and Dig Safe notification.

## Gas permit

- Newton ISD administers gas permits and requires the appropriate signed license documentation for gas-permit applications. (Source: [Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/plumbing-gas-and-electrical))

## Excavation and utility coordination

- Massachusetts requires Dig Safe notification before covered excavation, and Newton Engineering may need to review work involving streets or utility trenches. (Source: [Massachusetts About Dig Safe](https://www.mass.gov/guides/about-dig-safe); [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))

## Questions and answers

### Does a gas-line installation require only a utility-company approval?

No. Newton has its own gas-permit process through ISD. Other City and state approvals may also apply depending on excavation and project scope. (Source: [Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/plumbing-gas-and-electrical))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the project is a new service, an extension, or a replacement
- Which gas utility must coordinate the service work
- Required gas permit documents and license information
- Whether trenching, street opening, or dewatering permits are required
- Pressure-test and inspection requirements
- Whether the project needs a separate electrical or building permit
- Contact: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services)
