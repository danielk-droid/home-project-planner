# Do I need a permit to replace central air conditioning in Newton, MA?

> Replacing central air equipment can involve regulated equipment and electrical work. Newton ISD administers electrical permits and other permits for regulated equipment. The official sources reviewed do not establish a universal mechanical-permit rule or one outdoor-unit setback for every replacement.

## Equipment and electrical permits

- Newton ISD issues permits for regulated equipment and administers separate electrical permit applications. (Source: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services); [Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/plumbing-gas-and-electrical))

## Questions and answers

### Does a like-for-like central AC replacement always need a mechanical permit?

The sources reviewed do not establish a universal answer. Confirm the exact equipment replacement, duct changes, electrical work, and inspection requirements with Newton ISD. (Source: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the project needs a mechanical, sheet-metal, electrical, or building permit
- Whether ductwork, refrigerant piping, or structural supports will change
- The zoning location and setback for the outdoor unit
- Whether the property is in a Local Historic District or is a Landmark
- Applicable current energy-code requirements
- Contact: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services)
