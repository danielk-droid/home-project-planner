# What are the zoning and permit limits for roof dormers in Newton, MA?

> A dormer that changes a roof or building structure should be reviewed by Newton ISD and checked against the property's zoning rules. Newton defines a half-story using the amount of floor area with a ceiling height of at least 7 feet under a sloping roof. The official sources reviewed do not establish one universal dormer-width limit for all Newton houses.

## Building permit

- The state building code requires a permit for regulated construction and alteration of a building. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## Half-story treatment

- Newton's half-story definition uses the area with a ceiling height of 7 feet or greater under a sloping roof compared with the floor area of the story below. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Definitions](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638887019852300000))

## Questions and answers

### Does a dormer automatically make an attic a full story?

Not automatically. The zoning definition depends on the qualifying ceiling-height area and the area of the story below. The design must be measured against the current ordinance. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Definitions](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638887019852300000))

## What to confirm with the City

- The applicable dormer-width and roof-form rules for the zoning district
- Whether the dormer changes the half-story calculation
- Whether the dormer affects building height or a required setback
- Whether the work changes the home's floor-area ratio
- Whether historic review applies
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
