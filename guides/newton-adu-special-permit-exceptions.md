# When do you need a special permit for an ADU in Newton, MA?

> Newton's standard by-right ADU limit is 1,000 square feet or 50% of the principal dwelling's total gross floor area, whichever is smaller. Newton lists a special-permit path for a detached ADU up to 1,500 square feet and for certain internal units up to 1,200 square feet. The exact pathway depends on the unit type and the property conditions.

## Standard by-right ADU size

- Newton allows a standard new internal or detached ADU by right up to 1,000 square feet or 50% of the primary residence's total floor area, whichever is smaller. (Source: [Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/zoning-and-development/zoning-updates/accessory-dwelling-units-adu))

## Special-permit paths

- Newton identifies detached ADUs up to 1,500 square feet and certain internal ADUs up to 1,200 square feet as special-permit cases. (Source: [Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/zoning-and-development/zoning-updates/accessory-dwelling-units-adu))

## Questions and answers

### Does every ADU larger than 1,000 square feet need the same type of special permit?

No. Newton distinguishes between internal and detached units and lists conditions for some units between 1,000 and 1,200 square feet. Confirm the proposed unit type and property history. (Source: [Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/zoning-and-development/zoning-updates/accessory-dwelling-units-adu))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the proposed ADU is internal or detached
- Whether the property or existing accessory structure meets the four-year condition
- Whether owner occupancy is a condition of the requested pathway
- The size calculation and principal-dwelling gross floor area
- Whether the City Council may impose additional conditions
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
