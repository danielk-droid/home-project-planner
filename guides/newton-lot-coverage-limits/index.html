What is the maximum lot coverage in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 2 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

For lots created on or after December 7, 1953, maximum lot coverage is 15% in SR1, 20% in SR2 and 30% in SR3. Older lots allow 20%, 30% and 30%. MR districts allow 30% for detached single- and two-family homes (Ord. Sec. 3.1.3 and 3.2.3).

Official source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30 ↗

Maximum lot coverage

  • SR1 / SR2 / SR3, lots created on or after 12/7/1953: 15% / 20% / 30%. (Zoning Ord. ↗)
  • SR1 / SR2 / SR3, lots created before 12/7/1953: 20% / 30% / 30%. (Zoning Ord. ↗)
  • MR1–MR4, detached single- or two-family, lots created on or after 12/7/1953: 30% in each district. (Zoning Ord. ↗)
  • MR1–MR3, lots created before 12/7/1953: 30%. The table gives no MR4 value for these older lots. (Zoning Ord. ↗)

Questions and answers

What is the lot coverage limit in SR2?
20% for lots created on or after December 7, 1953, and 30% for older lots (Sec. 3.1.3). Source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30 ↗
Is lot coverage the same as FAR?
No. FAR is a separate limit: gross floor area of all buildings divided by lot area (Sec. 1.5.5.A.1). Source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30 ↗
What is the limit in MR districts?
30% for detached single- and two-family homes (Sec. 3.2.3). Source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30 ↗
