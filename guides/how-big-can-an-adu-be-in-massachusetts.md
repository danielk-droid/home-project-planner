# How big can an ADU be in Massachusetts?

> Under state law, a protected-use ADU can be no larger than half the gross floor area of the main home or 900 square feet, whichever is smaller (M.G.L. c.40A §1A and 760 CMR 71.02). State guidance counts basements, lofts and other floored levels in gross floor area. Some towns, including Newton, allow larger ADUs under their own rules.

## The state cap

- The statutory definition caps ADU gross floor area at half the principal dwelling's gross floor area or 900 sq ft, whichever is smaller. (Source: [M.G.L. c.40A §1A](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleVII/Chapter40A/Section1A))
- The state regulation repeats the same smaller-of-half-or-900-square-feet limit. (Source: [760 CMR 71.00 — Protected Use ADUs](https://www.mass.gov/doc/760-cmr-71-protected-use-accessory-dwelling-units/download))
- State guidance says gross floor area includes basements, lofts and intermediate floored levels, and it lists some spaces that are excluded. (Source: [Accessory Dwelling Unit FAQs (Mass.gov)](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/accessory-dwelling-unit-adu-faqs))

## Newton's local limits

- Newton's baseline for both internal and detached ADUs is 1,000 sq ft or 50% of the principal dwelling's gross floor area, whichever is less. A special permit can allow up to 1,200 sq ft for internal ADUs and 1,500 sq ft for detached ADUs (Ord. Sec. 6.7.1.D–E). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

## Questions and answers

### What is the maximum ADU size under Massachusetts law?

The smaller of half the main home's gross floor area or 900 square feet. (Source: [M.G.L. c.40A §1A](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleVII/Chapter40A/Section1A))

### Does my basement count toward gross floor area?

State guidance says gross floor area includes basements, lofts and intermediate floored levels. (Source: [Accessory Dwelling Unit FAQs (Mass.gov)](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/accessory-dwelling-unit-adu-faqs))

### Can a Newton ADU be bigger than 900 sq ft?

Newton's ordinance sets a 1,000 sq ft / 50% baseline, with larger sizes by special permit (Sec. 6.7.1). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
