# How do you get approval for a new or widened curb cut in Newton, MA?

> Newton Engineering reviews modifications to curb cuts as part of its site-plan and public-way review. A new or widened driveway opening should be discussed with Engineering before construction. The sources reviewed do not establish a single maximum width or intersection-spacing number for every residential property.

## Engineering review

- Newton Engineering reviews site plans that include modifying curb cuts. (Source: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))

## Related zoning rules

- Newton zoning contains rules for parking spaces and driveway arrangements. Those rules should not be treated as a universal curb-cut-width standard. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 5.1](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/639035614023930000))

## Questions and answers

### Is the residential curb-cut maximum always 20 feet?

The cited zoning rule about a 20-foot maximum applies to entrance and exit drives for certain parking facilities, not necessarily every residential curb opening. Confirm the curb-cut standard directly with Engineering. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638055970231470000); [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))

## What to confirm with the City

- Maximum width for the proposed residential curb opening
- Required spacing from intersections, hydrants, and nearby curb cuts
- Whether the driveway crosses a sidewalk or public right-of-way
- Required site plan, sight-distance, drainage, or restoration documents
- Whether a zoning or historic approval is needed
- Contact: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering)
