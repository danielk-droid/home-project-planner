SOLAR

Do I need a permit for solar panels in Newton, MA?

SHORT ANSWER

Yes. Newton's permit fee schedule lists a building permit category for solar panels without structural work, a separate category for solar panels with structural work, and an electrical permit category for solar.

Official source: Permit Fee Schedule ↗

What the City lists

  • The fee schedule lists a building-permit category for solar panels without structural work. (Fee schedule ↗)
  • It separately lists structural-work building permits for solar panels. (Fee schedule ↗)
  • It lists an electrical permit category for solar panels. (Fee schedule ↗)
  • Electrical permit applications are filed online and require an appropriately signed electrical license. (Electrical permits ↗)

Questions and answers

Does a solar installation need an electrical permit in Newton?
The fee schedule lists an electrical permit category for solar panels. Source: Permit Fee Schedule ↗
Is a building permit needed if there's no structural work?
The fee schedule lists a building permit category for solar panels without structural work. Source: Permit Fee Schedule ↗
Do historic district rules apply to solar?
Exterior changes in local historic districts require HDC review. Source: Historic Preservation ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Whether your roof needs structural work.
  • Current fees for each category.
