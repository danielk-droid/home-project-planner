# How tall can a house be in Newton, MA?

> In Newton's SR and MR districts, a detached single- or two-family house may be up to 36 feet tall with a sloped roof or 30 feet with a flat roof. The limit is 2.5 stories, or 3 by special permit (Ord. Sec. 3.1.3 and 3.2.3).

## Limits

- SR1–SR3 detached single-family: 36' sloped roof, 30' flat roof (Sec. 3.1.3). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- SR1–SR3: 2.5 stories, or 3 stories by special permit. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- MR1–MR4 detached single- or two-family: 36' sloped, 30' flat, 2.5 stories or 3 by special permit (Sec. 3.2.3). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

## Measurement

- Height is measured vertically from the average grade plane to the highest point of the roof, subject to listed exclusions (Sec. 1.5.4.A.1). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

## Questions and answers

### What is the height limit for a house in Newton?

36 feet with a sloped roof and 30 feet with a flat roof in SR and MR districts. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### How many stories can I build?

2.5 stories, or 3 by special permit. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### How is height measured?

From the average grade plane to the highest roof point, subject to exclusions listed in Sec. 1.5.4. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

- Whether your attic counts as a half story.
