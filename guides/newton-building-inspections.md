# How do building inspections work in Newton, MA?

> The permit holder arranges every inspection. For building inspections, call before 8 AM on the day the work is ready, and the inspector calls back by 9:30 AM to schedule it. Electrical and plumbing/gas inspections are scheduled in advance, and the final building inspection comes last.

## Scheduling

- The permit holder must arrange all inspections. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))
- Building inspections are same-day when the job is complete and ready. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))
- Call before 8 AM on the desired day, and leave a voicemail if no one answers. The inspector calls back by 9:30 AM to schedule. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))
- Electrical and plumbing/gas inspections are scheduled in advance. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))

## What must be ready at each stage

- Excavation: inspected at the bottom of the hole, before footings or crushed stone. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))
- Footings: forms stripped before the wall is poured. Deck footings are inspected before pouring. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))
- Before backfill: foundation poured and coated if required. An as-built may be requested. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))
- Rough: building weather-tight with roof, windows and doors installed and siding 75% complete. Rough plumbing, electrical and HVAC inspections done first, and all firestopping complete. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))
- Insulation: insulation and vapor barrier installed, and a mid-point HERS rating uploaded to the portal. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))
- Final: last, after all other inspections pass. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))

## Questions and answers

### How do I schedule a building inspection in Newton?

Call before 8 AM on the day you want it. Leave a voicemail if no one answers, and the inspector calls back by 9:30 AM. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))

### Who is responsible for scheduling?

The permit holder. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))

### What is needed for a rough inspection?

A weather-tight building with roof, windows and doors installed, siding 75% complete, rough trade inspections passed, and firestopping complete. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))

### What is needed for an insulation inspection?

Completed insulation, an installed vapor barrier, and a mid-point HERS rating uploaded to the portal. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))

### When is the final inspection?

Last, after all other inspections pass. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))
