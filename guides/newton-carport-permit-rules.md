# Can I build a carport in Newton, MA, and what permits are required?

> A new permanent carport should be reviewed by Newton ISD as a building or structure project and checked against the property's accessory-building or principal-building zoning rules. Newton's accessory-building setbacks generally include 5 feet from side and rear lot lines and 5 feet from the principal building. The exact treatment depends on the carport's design and location.

## Building permit review

- The state building code requires a permit before regulated construction of a building or structure. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## Accessory-building location

- Newton's accessory-building ordinance generally requires setbacks from side and rear lot lines and separation from the principal building. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638055970231470000))

## Questions and answers

### Is an open-sided carport automatically exempt from building and zoning rules?

The cited sources do not establish a general carport exemption. Confirm how Newton classifies the proposed permanent structure and which permits apply. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download); [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638055970231470000))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the carport is classified as an accessory building or part of the principal building
- Required height, setbacks, and maximum footprint
- Structural design requirements for wind and snow loads
- Driveway and parking layout compliance
- Whether zoning relief or a special permit is required
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
