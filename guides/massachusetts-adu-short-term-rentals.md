# Can an ADU be used for Airbnb or short-term rentals in Massachusetts?

> Massachusetts state law allows municipalities to impose restrictions or prohibitions on short-term rentals of ADUs. Newton says its ADUs cannot be used as short-term rentals and must be rented for a period of more than 30 days. Confirm whether any separate rental registration or other housing requirements apply.

## State authority

- Massachusetts law allows local restrictions and prohibitions on short-term rentals of ADUs. (Source: [Massachusetts General Laws, Chapter 40A, Section 3](https://malegislature.gov/laws/generallaws/parti/titlevii/chapter40a/section3))

## Newton's local rule

- Newton states that ADUs are prohibited from being used as short-term rentals and must be rented for more than 30 days. (Source: [Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/zoning-and-development/zoning-updates/accessory-dwelling-units-adu))

## Questions and answers

### Can I list a Newton ADU on Airbnb for weekend stays?

Newton's published ADU guidance says ADUs cannot be used as short-term rentals and must be rented for more than 30 days. (Source: [Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/zoning-and-development/zoning-updates/accessory-dwelling-units-adu))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the proposed rental term meets Newton's minimum period
- Whether any separate rental registration, inspection, or tax rules apply
- Whether the property has a pre-February 12, 2025 ADU approval with different conditions
- Whether the ADU is legally permitted and has required occupancy approval
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
