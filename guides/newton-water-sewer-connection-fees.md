# How do water and sewer connection or upgrade permits work in Newton, MA?

> For a new water service, Newton requires the property owner to submit a water-service application to the Utilities Division. The City then prepares a cost estimate for its portion of the work. The private utility contractor must coordinate the excavation, obtain an Engineering permit, and have the work inspected. Sewer connection requirements and fees should be confirmed separately.

## Water-service application

- Newton requires a water-service application for a new water service and says the Utilities Division prepares a cost estimate for City labor and materials. (Source: [Newton Updating and Repairing Water and Sewer Services](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/updating-and-repairing-water-sewer-services))

## Engineering permit and inspection

- Newton states that the private utility contractor must obtain an Engineering permit and have the work inspected by the Engineering Division. (Source: [Newton Updating and Repairing Water and Sewer Services](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/updating-and-repairing-water-sewer-services))

## Questions and answers

### Does the City provide a fixed price for every new water service?

No. Newton says the Utilities Division generates a cost estimate for the proposed work after the water-service application is submitted. (Source: [Newton Updating and Repairing Water and Sewer Services](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/updating-and-repairing-water-sewer-services))

## What to confirm with the City

- The current water-service application and estimate
- Whether sewer service is being added, repaired, or enlarged
- Current water and sewer connection or upgrade fees
- Backflow-prevention requirements for the proposed connection
- Required trench, street-opening, and restoration permits
- Inspection and as-built documentation
- Contact: [Newton Public Works, Utilities Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works)
