# Do ADUs in Newton need separate water, sewer, and electric meters?

> The official sources reviewed do not establish a universal requirement for every Newton ADU to have separate water, sewer, or electric meters. Newton's Utilities Division handles water-service applications and prepares a project-specific cost estimate for new water service. Confirm meter, sewer, and electrical-service requirements for the particular ADU before construction.

## Water service

- Newton requires a water-service application for a new water service, after which the Utilities Division prepares a cost estimate for the work described by the City. (Source: [Newton Updating and Repairing Water and Sewer Services](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/updating-and-repairing-water-sewer-services))

## Trade permits

- Newton ISD administers separate plumbing, gas, and electrical trade permits where that work requires a permit. (Source: [Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/plumbing-gas-and-electrical))

## Questions and answers

### Must every ADU have its own water meter?

The reviewed official sources do not establish a universal separate-meter rule. Ask Newton Utilities and ISD about the proposed unit and existing service configuration. (Source: [Newton Updating and Repairing Water and Sewer Services](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/updating-and-repairing-water-sewer-services); [Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/plumbing-gas-and-electrical))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the ADU may share existing water, sewer, and electric services
- Whether separate or submetered utilities are required or permitted
- Water-service connection, tapping, and inspection costs
- Any sewer-capacity, connection, or tie-in fees
- Electrical service capacity and utility approval
- Backflow prevention or other water-service conditions
- Contact: [Newton Public Works, Utilities Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works)
