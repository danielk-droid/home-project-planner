# How long does a City Council or ZBA special permit take in Newton, MA?

> Massachusetts law generally requires a special-permit public hearing within 65 days after the application is filed and a decision within 90 days after the public hearing. The time limits may be extended by written agreement. Newton's City Council is the special-permit granting authority for the City's zoning process, and the full project timeline may be longer because application preparation and review occur before the hearing.

## State hearing deadline

- Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 40A, Section 9 sets a general 65-day deadline for the special-permit public hearing after filing. (Source: [https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleVII/Chapter40A/Section9](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleVII/Chapter40A/Section9))

## State decision deadline

- The statute sets a general 90-day period for the decision after the public hearing, subject to a written extension agreement. (Source: [https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleVII/Chapter40A/Section9](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleVII/Chapter40A/Section9))

## Newton's review process

- Newton's special-permit process involves an application to the City Council and a public hearing before the Land Use Committee. (Source: [Newton Special Permits and Land Use](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/development-review/special-permits-land-use))

## Questions and answers

### Does the 65-day deadline mean a Newton special permit is always finished within 65 days?

No. The 65-day period is the general statutory deadline for holding the public hearing after filing. The decision period follows the hearing, and written extensions may be agreed. (Source: [https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleVII/Chapter40A/Section9](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleVII/Chapter40A/Section9))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the application is complete and accepted for filing
- The current Land Use Committee meeting calendar
- Whether a written extension agreement is anticipated
- Whether the project requires a separate site-plan or other permit
- The decision filing date and appeal deadline
- Contact: [Newton Special Permits and Land Use](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/development-review/special-permits-land-use)
