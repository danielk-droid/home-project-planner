# How does the Massachusetts HIC arbitration and Guaranty Fund protect homeowners?

> Massachusetts provides an HIC arbitration and Guaranty Fund process for eligible homeowners who have a qualifying dispute with a registered home-improvement contractor. The Guaranty Fund recovery is capped at $10,000 for actual loss. State law also requires covered home-improvement contracts over $1,000 to be in writing.

## Guaranty Fund limit

- Massachusetts regulations state that an eligible claimant may recover once up to, and no more than, $10,000 from the Guaranty Fund for actual loss. (Source: [Massachusetts HIC Arbitration and Guaranty Fund Regulation](https://www.mass.gov/doc/home-improvement-contractor-arbitration-and-guaranty-fund/download))

## Written contracts

- Massachusetts requires home-improvement contracts over $1,000 to be in writing. (Source: [Massachusetts Home Improvement Contract Sample Language](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/home-improvement-contract-sample-language))

## Questions and answers

### Can every homeowner recover $10,000 from the HIC Guaranty Fund?

No. The fund has eligibility requirements, and the $10,000 figure is a maximum recovery for eligible claims, not an automatic payment. (Source: [Massachusetts HIC Arbitration and Guaranty Fund Regulation](https://www.mass.gov/doc/home-improvement-contractor-arbitration-and-guaranty-fund/download))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the contractor was properly registered when the work was contracted
- Whether the homeowner and property meet the Guaranty Fund eligibility conditions
- Whether arbitration is required or appropriate before a fund claim
- Whether the homeowner pulled the building permit instead of the contractor
- Current filing deadlines and claim documentation
- Contact: [Massachusetts Office of Consumer Affairs and Business Regulation](https://www.mass.gov/orgs/office-of-consumer-affairs-and-business-regulation)
