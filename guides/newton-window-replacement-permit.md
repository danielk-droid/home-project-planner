# Do I need a building permit to replace windows in Newton, MA?

> The state building code exempts ordinary repairs, but a project that cuts or changes structural supports may require a building permit. Newton also requires historic review for window changes at properties in its Local Historic Districts. The exact permit treatment of a same-size replacement outside a historic area should be confirmed with ISD.

## Same-opening replacement and structural changes

- The state code exempts ordinary repairs but requires a permit for specified structural work, including substantial cutting of structural members or removal of load-bearing support. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## Historic windows

- Newton's Local Historic District application process covers exterior changes. The city provides a specific window-replacement checklist for historic district applications. (Source: [Newton Historic Preservation, Submit an Application](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/historic-preservation/submit-an-application))

## Questions and answers

### Can I change a window opening without checking for a permit?

No. A change to the opening or its structural support may fall outside the ordinary-repair exemption. Confirm the plans with ISD before work begins. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the replacement keeps the existing opening and structural framing
- The applicable emergency escape and rescue opening requirements for a bedroom
- Whether the property is in a Local Historic District or is a Landmark
- Whether window materials, dimensions, or visible appearance need historic approval
- Contact: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services)
