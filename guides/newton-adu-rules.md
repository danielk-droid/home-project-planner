# What are the ADU rules in Newton, MA?

> Newton allows one ADU per lot on a single- or two-family property, in any zoning district. No extra parking is required, the ADU can't be sold separately, and rentals must be at least 30 days. The baseline size is 1,000 sq ft or 50% of the home's floor area, whichever is less, and a special permit can allow more.

## Core rules (Ord. Sec. 6.7.1)

- A single- or two-family home may add one ADU regardless of zoning district, according to Newton Planning. (Source: [Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/zoning-and-development/zoning-updates/accessory-dwelling-units-adu))
- No more than one ADU per lot, and no separate ownership (Sec. 6.7.1.C.1–2). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- No additional parking is required (Sec. 6.7.1.C.5). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- Minimum rental or occupancy term of 30 days for either dwelling (Sec. 6.7.1.C.4). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

## Size

- Internal ADU: up to 1,000 sq ft or 50% of the total gross floor area, whichever is less. The total counts both units (Sec. 6.7.1.D.2). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- Internal ADU by special permit: up to 1,200 sq ft (Sec. 6.7.1.D.2.b). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- Newton Planning describes a conditional by-right allowance for internal ADUs of up to the smaller of 1,200 sq ft or 50%, for a residence whose certificate of occupancy is at least four years old. (Source: [Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/zoning-and-development/zoning-updates/accessory-dwelling-units-adu))
- Detached ADU: up to 1,000 sq ft or 50%, whichever is less, or up to 1,500 sq ft by special permit (Sec. 6.7.1.E.2). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

## Questions and answers

### Can I build an ADU in Newton?

Newton Planning says a single- or two-family home may add one ADU regardless of zoning district. (Source: [Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/zoning-and-development/zoning-updates/accessory-dwelling-units-adu))

### Do I need extra parking for a Newton ADU?

No. Sec. 6.7.1.C.5 requires no additional parking. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### Can I Airbnb my ADU in Newton?

The ordinance sets a minimum rental or occupancy term of 30 days (Sec. 6.7.1.C.4). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### How big can a detached ADU be in Newton?

1,000 sq ft or 50% of the total gross floor area, whichever is less, or up to 1,500 sq ft by special permit. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### Can I sell the ADU separately?

No. The ordinance prohibits separate ownership (Sec. 6.7.1.C.1). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### Who answers ADU questions in Newton?

Newton Planning lists an ADU questions email (adu@newtonma.gov) on its ADU page. (Source: [Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/zoning-and-development/zoning-updates/accessory-dwelling-units-adu))
