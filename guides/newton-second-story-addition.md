# What zoning and building code rules apply to adding a second story in Newton, MA?

> A second-story addition requires building-code review and a building permit. Newton zoning controls the height, setbacks, and floor-area ratio of the expanded principal building. Whether the design is allowed by right or needs a special permit depends on the property's zoning district and existing conditions.

## Building and structural review

- The state building code requires a permit before regulated construction or alteration of a building. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## Zoning standards

- Newton's special-permit process covers projects that need approval under the zoning ordinance, including certain additions and nonconforming structures. (Source: [Newton Special Permits and Land Use](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/development-review/special-permits-land-use))

## Questions and answers

### Can every Newton home add a second story within a 36-foot height limit?

No citywide answer is established by that single number. Height, story count, roof form, and floor-area limits depend on the property's district and applicable zoning provisions. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638055970231470000))

## What to confirm with the City

- The zoning district and applicable principal-building height limit
- Existing and proposed floor-area ratio
- Required front, side, and rear setbacks
- Whether the existing foundation and framing can support the addition
- Whether special-permit or nonconforming-structure review applies
- Required architect, engineer, and building permit documents
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
