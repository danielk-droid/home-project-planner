# How do I apply for and track a permit on Newton's online portal?

> Newton's Inspectional Services takes applications online through Apply for Permits, which requires an OpenGov account. The City also provides a public search by address or record number, which you can use to confirm permits are closed before you make the final payment to a contractor.

## Using the portal

- Use the City's Apply for Permits link to start or manage an application. (Source: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services))
- An OpenGov account is required for online applications. (Source: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services))
- The City provides a public search link for address or record-number searches. (Source: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services))
- Check the permitting website to confirm permits are closed before you pay the contractor in full. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))

## Questions and answers

### Do I need an account to apply?

Yes. The City says an OpenGov account is required for online applications. (Source: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services))

### Can I look up permits on my address?

Yes. The City provides a public search by address or record number. (Source: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services))

### When should I check my permit status?

The City advises checking that permits are closed before you make the final payment to a contractor. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))

### Who can I call for help?

Inspectional Services at (617) 796-1060, City Hall Room 202. (Source: [Inspectional Services — Contact Us](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/contact-us))

## What to confirm with the City
