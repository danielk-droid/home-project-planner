# What rules apply to building or renovating a home in a FEMA flood zone in Newton, MA?

> Newton identifies FEMA floodplain and City floodplain areas as locations where land-altering work must be reviewed by the Conservation Commission. The exact building elevation, flood-resistant construction, and substantial-improvement requirements depend on the property's mapped flood zone and proposed work. Do not rely on a general 50% rule without confirming the applicable code and floodplain provisions.

## Newton floodplain review

- Newton's wetlands guidance identifies FEMA Floodplain and City Floodplain as areas where land-altering work must be reviewed and permitted by the Conservation Commission. (Source: [Newton Wetlands Permitting Information](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/conservation-office/wetlands-permitting-rev))

## Building-code requirements

- The Massachusetts State Building Code includes flood-protection provisions, and the applicable requirements depend on the type and location of the building. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR](https://www.mass.gov/massachusetts-state-building-code-780-cmr))

## Questions and answers

### Does the 50% substantial-improvement threshold answer every Newton floodplain question?

No. The sources reviewed do not establish that one threshold resolves every case. Confirm the mapped flood zone, current code provision, and City floodplain ordinance for the specific property. (Source: [Newton Wetlands Permitting Information](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/conservation-office/wetlands-permitting-rev); [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR](https://www.mass.gov/massachusetts-state-building-code-780-cmr))

## What to confirm with the City

- The property's FEMA flood zone and base flood elevation
- Whether the parcel is also within a City floodplain
- Applicable substantial-improvement and substantial-damage definitions
- Required lowest-floor and equipment elevations
- Flood-resistant construction requirements
- Whether a Conservation Commission filing or engineering plan is needed
- Contact: [Newton Conservation Commission](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/conservation-office)
