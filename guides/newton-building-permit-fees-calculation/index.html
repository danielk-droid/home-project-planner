PERMIT FEES

How does Newton calculate building permit fees for residential renovations?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 2 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

A Newton ISD fee schedule lists building and trade permit fees based on estimated construction cost, with a minimum residential permit fee. However, the schedule identifies an original effective date of July 1, 2015, so homeowners should confirm the current fee and any later changes directly with ISD before relying on the listed amounts.

Official source: Permit Fee Schedule ↗ Inspectional Services Department ↗

Published fee method

  • Newton's published fee document describes a fee calculated from estimated construction cost and lists a minimum residential fee. (Fee schedule ↗)

Check current rates

  • The online fee document states an original effective date of July 1, 2015, so its rate should be confirmed with ISD before use in a current estimate. (Fee schedule ↗)

Questions and answers

Can I use the posted fee schedule as a guaranteed current permit price?
No. The source carries an older effective date. Ask Newton ISD for the current fee calculation and any additional plan-review or inspection charges. Source: Permit Fee Schedule ↗, Inspectional Services Department ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • The current residential base fee and cost-based fee rate
  • Whether plan-review fees apply
  • Whether electrical, plumbing, gas, or other trade permits have separate fees
  • Reinspection or certificate fees
  • Whether fees are calculated on labor, materials, or total project cost

Contact: Inspectional Services Department ↗
