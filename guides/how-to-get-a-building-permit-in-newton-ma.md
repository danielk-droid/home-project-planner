# How do I get a building permit in Newton, MA?

> Apply online through the City's OpenGov permitting system, using the City's Building Permit Application Checklist to assemble signatures, licensing and insurance documents, and plans. Inspectors review the application and issue the permit, and every permit needs a final inspection.

## Steps the City lists

- Start from Apply for Permits on the City's building-permits page. (Source: [Building Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/building-permits))
- Create or sign in to an OpenGov account to submit and manage the application. (Source: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services))
- Complete the application with contractor and homeowner signatures. (Source: [Building Permit Application Checklist](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29379/638630352968235564))
- Include licensing, insurance, and homeowner-exemption documents as the checklist requires. (Source: [Building Permit Application Checklist](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29379/638630352968235564))
- Prepare plans showing elevations, floor plans, foundation, and framing. (Source: [Building Permit Application Checklist](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29379/638630352968235564))
- Check zoning, and include a recorded special permit or variance when one applies. (Source: [Building Permit Application Checklist](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29379/638630352968235564))
- Inspectors review applications, then approve and issue permits. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))
- Arrange the required inspections. All permits require a final inspection. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))

## Fees

- The published fee schedule charges $20 per $1,000 of estimated construction cost, with fractions rounded up to the next thousand. (Source: [Permit Fee Schedule](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29405/639029478067930000))

## Questions and answers

### Can I apply for a Newton building permit online?

Yes. The City's building-permits page links to Apply for Permits, and online applications require an OpenGov account. (Source: [Building Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/building-permits); [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services))

### How much does a building permit cost in Newton?

The City fee schedule lists $20 per $1,000 of estimated construction cost, with fractions rounded up to the next thousand. Check the current schedule for your permit type. (Source: [Permit Fee Schedule](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29405/639029478067930000))

### What plans do I need?

The City checklist calls for plans showing elevations, floor plans, foundation, and framing. (Source: [Building Permit Application Checklist](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29379/638630352968235564))

### Do I need zoning approval first?

The checklist asks applicants to check zoning and provide a recorded special permit or variance when applicable. (Source: [Building Permit Application Checklist](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29379/638630352968235564))

### Who schedules inspections?

The permit holder arranges all inspections, and every permit requires a final inspection. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))

### Where is Inspectional Services?

City Hall, 1000 Commonwealth Ave, second floor, Room 202. Phone (617) 796-1060. (Source: [Inspectional Services — Contact Us](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/contact-us))
