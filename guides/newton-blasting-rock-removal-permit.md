# What permits and notifications are required for rock blasting in Newton, MA?

> The exact Newton permit sequence and notification requirements for residential rock blasting must be confirmed directly with the City's relevant departments. Massachusetts Dig Safe law requires advance notice for covered excavation and requires blasting to be identified in the notice. Do not attempt blasting yourself; obtain direct guidance from Newton Fire Prevention and the responsible City offices.

## State excavation notice

- Massachusetts Dig Safe law requires covered excavators to provide advance notice and identify whether the excavation will involve blasting. (Source: [Massachusetts Dig Safe Law, G.L. c. 82, Sections 40 and 40A](https://www.mass.gov/doc/the-dig-safe-law-mgl-c-82-ss-40-40a-40e/download))

## City review

- Newton Fire Prevention is the City's official Fire Department resource for fire-related permits and requirements. (Source: [Newton Fire Department, Fire Prevention](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/fire/fire-prevention))

## Questions and answers

### Does a Dig Safe ticket replace any City approval for rock removal?

No. Dig Safe notification is a state excavation requirement; it does not establish that all separate City approvals have been obtained. (Source: [Massachusetts About Dig Safe](https://www.mass.gov/guides/about-dig-safe); [Newton Fire Department, Fire Prevention](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/fire/fire-prevention))

## What to confirm with the City

- The specific City permits and approvals required for the proposed project
- Whether Fire Prevention, Engineering, ISD, or other departments must review the work
- Required advance notifications and property-specific approvals
- Whether pre-project property documentation or monitoring is required
- The applicable excavation, noise, and site-restoration conditions
- Contact: [Newton Fire Department, Fire Prevention](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/fire/fire-prevention)
