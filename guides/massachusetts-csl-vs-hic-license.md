# What is the difference between a Massachusetts CSL and HIC license?

> A Massachusetts Construction Supervisor License (CSL) concerns supervision of construction work regulated by the building code. Home Improvement Contractor (HIC) registration is a separate consumer-protection registration for contractors doing covered home-improvement work. A project may involve both requirements, so homeowners should not treat one credential as a substitute for the other.

## Construction Supervisor License

- Massachusetts says certain construction projects require oversight by a licensed construction supervisor or registered design professional. (Source: [Massachusetts When Is a Construction Supervisor License Required?](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/when-is-a-construction-supervisor-license-required))

## Home Improvement Contractor registration

- Massachusetts HIC rules separately regulate contractor registration, written contracts, permits, payments, and access to the Guaranty Fund. (Source: [Massachusetts HIC Contractor Resources](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/hic-contractor-resources))

## Questions and answers

### Does an HIC registration replace a CSL?

No. The CSL and HIC programs address different requirements: construction supervision under the building code and contractor registration under the home-improvement law. (Source: [Massachusetts When Is a Construction Supervisor License Required?](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/when-is-a-construction-supervisor-license-required); [Massachusetts HIC Contractor Resources](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/hic-contractor-resources))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the project requires a CSL or registered design professional
- Whether the contractor must hold active HIC registration
- Whether the contractor's registration and license match the work being performed
- Whether the homeowner is relying on an applicable owner exemption
- Whether the proposed contract meets Massachusetts home-improvement contract rules
- Contact: [Massachusetts Office of Consumer Affairs and Business Regulation](https://www.mass.gov/orgs/office-of-consumer-affairs-and-business-regulation)
