# Do I need a building permit to replace a roof in Newton, MA?

> The Massachusetts building code exempts ordinary repairs from a building permit, but work involving substantial removal or alteration of structural supports is not treated as an ordinary repair. Newton has a specific roofing and siding permit application category, so homeowners should confirm the current application requirement for their exact roof scope.

## Ordinary repairs and structural work

- The state building code says an application or notice is not required for ordinary repairs, but separately identifies substantial cutting of structural members and removal or alteration of load-bearing supports as work requiring a permit. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## Newton's permit categories

- Newton ISD has separated permit applications into specific work categories, including roofing and siding. (Source: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services))

## Questions and answers

### Does every roof replacement require a building permit in Newton?

The sources reviewed do not establish a universal rule for every roof replacement method. Confirm whether the proposed stripping, overlay, decking, or structural work requires a Newton building permit. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download); [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the project is ordinary repair, full replacement, or structural alteration
- Whether roof decking, rafters, trusses, or other structural members will be changed
- Whether an overlay is allowed for the existing roof condition
- The current Newton roofing application and inspection requirements
- Whether the property is in a Local Historic District or is a Landmark
- Contact: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services)
