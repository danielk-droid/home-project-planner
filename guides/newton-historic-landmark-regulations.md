# How do landmark designation rules work for historic homes in Newton, MA?

> A Newton City Landmark requires historic review before exterior work begins. A property with a Preservation Restriction also requires review under the terms of that restriction. The required approval and design standards depend on the individual property's designation and the scope of the proposed work.

## Local Landmark review

- Newton requires a Local Landmark Review Application for changes to a designated City Landmark, with Historical Commission approval before exterior work begins. (Source: [Newton Historical Commission](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/historic-preservation/newton-historical-commission))

## Preservation Restrictions

- Newton says the terms of each Preservation Restriction specify what work may or may not be allowed and require review before exterior work. (Source: [Newton Historical Commission](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/historic-preservation/newton-historical-commission))

## Questions and answers

### Does landmark approval replace a building permit?

No. Historic approval and building permits are separate approvals. Newton says a historic certificate is necessary but not sufficient for a building permit. (Source: [Newton Historic Preservation Certificate Review Process](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/82943/637846791352200000))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the property is a City Landmark or has a Preservation Restriction
- Which review application applies
- Whether staff review or a full Commission hearing is required
- Whether the proposed work meets the property's design guidelines
- Whether other City permits are also required
- Contact: [Newton Historical Commission](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/historic-preservation/newton-historical-commission)
