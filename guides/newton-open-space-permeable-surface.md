# What are Newton's minimum open space and permeable surface requirements?

> Newton's required open space and maximum lot coverage vary by zoning district and lot creation date. For single-residence lots created on or after December 7, 1953, the ordinance lists minimum open space of 70% in SR1, 65% in SR2, and 50% in SR3. Older lots have a different table, so a single citywide percentage is not correct.

## Standards for newer single-residence lots

- For lots created on or after December 7, 1953, Newton's table lists minimum open space of 70% in SR1, 65% in SR2, and 50% in SR3. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Definitions](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638887019852300000))

## Older single-residence lots

- Newton uses a separate dimensional table for lots created before December 7, 1953. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Definitions](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638887019852300000))
- Newton Engineering reviews expansions of impervious area, including patios, pools, and driveways. (Source: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))

## Questions and answers

### Can I use the SR2 open-space percentage for every Newton property?

No. The applicable percentage depends on the zoning district and the lot's creation date. Other districts have their own standards. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Definitions](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638887019852300000))

## What to confirm with the City

- The property's zoning district and legal lot creation date
- How Newton classifies each existing and proposed surface
- The lot's current lot coverage and open-space calculations
- Whether a stormwater permit is required
- Whether the property is in a VCOD or another overlay district
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
