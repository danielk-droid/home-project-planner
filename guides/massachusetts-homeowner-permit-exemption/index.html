HOMEOWNER PERMITS

When can a Newton homeowner pull their own building permit?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 4 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

Massachusetts building-code rules provide a homeowner exemption from certain construction-supervisor licensing requirements when the homeowner performs the work and supervises hired workers. This is not a general exemption from building permits or other safety requirements. Newton homeowners should confirm permit eligibility and the HIC Guaranty Fund consequences before applying in their own name.

Official source: 780 CMR Tenth Edition, Chapter 110.R5, Licensing of Construction Supervisors ↗ Massachusetts HIC Homeowner Resources ↗

Homeowner licensing exemption

  • The Massachusetts construction-supervisor licensing regulation contains an exemption for a homeowner performing work for which a building permit is required, subject to the stated supervision conditions. (780 CMR Tenth Edition, Chapter 110.R5, L ↗)

Permit and consumer-protection consequences

  • Massachusetts says a homeowner who applies for the building permit may not be eligible for compensation from the HIC Guaranty Fund. (Massachusetts HIC Homeowner Resources ↗)

Questions and answers

Does the homeowner exemption mean I can do regulated work without a building permit?
No. The exemption concerns construction-supervisor licensing; it does not eliminate the underlying building permit or applicable code requirements. Source: 780 CMR Tenth Edition, Chapter 110.R5, Licensing of Construction Supervisors ↗, Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2 ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Whether the homeowner meets the exact owner-exemption requirements
  • Whether the building is a one- or two-family dwelling or another building type
  • Whether any work must be supervised by a CSL or registered design professional
  • Whether the homeowner's permit application affects HIC Guaranty Fund eligibility
  • Newton's current application requirements and permit-holder responsibilities

Contact: Inspectional Services Department ↗
