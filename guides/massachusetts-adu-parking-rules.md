# Does Massachusetts state law require parking for an ADU?

> Massachusetts law limits a municipality to requiring no more than one additional parking space for a single ADU in a single-family residential zoning district. No additional space may be required when the ADU is within 0.5 miles of a commuter rail station, subway station, ferry terminal, or bus station. Newton's local rules and the property's transit distance must still be checked.

## State parking protection

- Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 40A, Section 3 limits required additional parking for one ADU and removes the additional-space requirement within the specified half-mile transit distance. (Source: [Massachusetts General Laws, Chapter 40A, Section 3](https://malegislature.gov/laws/generallaws/parti/titlevii/chapter40a/section3))

## Local review

- The state ADU regulation makes the protected ADU subject to otherwise applicable dimensional and parking requirements within the limits of state law. (Source: [760 CMR 71.00, Protected Use Accessory Dwelling Units](https://www.mass.gov/regulations/760-CMR-7100-protected-use-accessory-dwelling-units))

## Questions and answers

### Can Newton require an extra ADU parking space within half a mile of transit?

State law says no additional parking space may be required for the protected ADU when it is within 0.5 miles of one of the listed transit facilities. (Source: [Massachusetts General Laws, Chapter 40A, Section 3](https://malegislature.gov/laws/generallaws/parti/titlevii/chapter40a/section3))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the ADU is the single protected ADU described in state law
- Whether the property is within 0.5 miles of a qualifying transit facility
- How the City's measurement is made for the particular parcel
- Whether any additional ADU or separate use changes the analysis
- Whether site-plan, access, or other lawful local requirements apply
