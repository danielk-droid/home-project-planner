# What permits are required for a swimming pool in Newton, MA?

> Newton requires a building permit before construction, installation, or enlargement of a private swimming pool. The city also requires an outdoor private pool to be enclosed by a fence or wall at least 5 feet high. Electrical, site, drainage, and zoning requirements may apply separately.

## Newton pool building permit

- Newton's private-pool ordinance prohibits starting construction, installation, or enlargement before the Inspectional Services Commissioner issues a building permit. (Source: [Newton Revised Ordinances, Chapter 5, Fence Regulations](https://www.newtonma.gov/Home/ShowDocument?id=29771))

## Outdoor pool enclosure

- Newton requires every outdoor private swimming pool to be enclosed by a fence or wall at least 5 feet high, whether or not the pool contains water. (Source: [Newton Revised Ordinances, Chapter 5, Fence Regulations](https://www.newtonma.gov/Home/ShowDocument?id=29771))
- Newton Engineering reviews site plans involving expansions of impervious area, including pools. (Source: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))

## Questions and answers

### Does an above-ground pool avoid Newton's pool permit rule?

Newton's cited ordinance applies to private swimming pools generally and requires a building permit before construction, installation, or enlargement. Confirm the requirements for the exact pool design with ISD. (Source: [Newton Revised Ordinances, Chapter 5, Fence Regulations](https://www.newtonma.gov/Home/ShowDocument?id=29771))

## What to confirm with the City

- Required gate, latch, opening, and enclosure details for the specific pool
- Applicable setbacks and location restrictions
- Electrical bonding and electrical permit requirements
- Any gas-heater or plumbing permits
- Stormwater, wetlands, floodplain, and site-plan requirements
- Contact: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services)
