# Do I need a permit for a fence in Newton, MA?

> Yes, a Newton fence permit is required for a perimeter fence greater than 4 feet tall. The state building code separately exempts fences not over 7 feet from its building-permit requirement, but that exemption does not remove Newton's local fence rules.

## Newton's local fence permit

- Newton requires a fence permit before erecting a perimeter fence greater than 4 feet tall. (Source: [Newton Revised Ordinances, Chapter 5, Fence Regulations](https://www.newtonma.gov/Home/ShowDocument?id=29771))
- For a fence bordering or parallel to a front lot line, the general height limit is 4 feet. A taller fence may be allowed with additional setbacks, subject to the ordinance's maximum height and openness rules. (Source: [Newton Revised Ordinances, Chapter 5, Fence Regulations](https://www.newtonma.gov/Home/ShowDocument?id=29771))

## State building-code exemption

- The tenth-edition Massachusetts building code exempts fences not over 7 feet from the building permit required under that code. This is separate from Newton's local fence permit. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## Questions and answers

### Does a fence under 7 feet automatically avoid all permits in Newton?

No. Newton's separate fence ordinance requires a local fence permit for a perimeter fence greater than 4 feet tall. (Source: [Newton Revised Ordinances, Chapter 5, Fence Regulations](https://www.newtonma.gov/Home/ShowDocument?id=29771))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the property is in a Local Historic District, is a Local Landmark, or has a Preservation Restriction
- The exact fence height and location allowed on a corner lot
- Whether wetlands, easements, or other site restrictions affect the proposed fence
- Whether the fence is a perimeter fence or a different type covered by a separate rule
- Contact: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services)
