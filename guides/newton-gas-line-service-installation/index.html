GAS LINES

What permits and process are required to add or upgrade a gas line in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 4 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

Gas piping work in Newton is handled through the ISD gas-permit process. Newton requires appropriate signed license documentation for gas permit applications. A project that involves excavation or a public right-of-way may also require Engineering review and Dig Safe notification.

Official source: Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical Permits ↗ Newton Engineering Division ↗ Massachusetts About Dig Safe ↗

Gas permit

  • Newton ISD administers gas permits and requires the appropriate signed license documentation for gas-permit applications. (Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical ↗)

Excavation and utility coordination

  • Massachusetts requires Dig Safe notification before covered excavation, and Newton Engineering may need to review work involving streets or utility trenches. (Massachusetts About Dig Safe ↗, Newton Engineering Division ↗)

Questions and answers

Does a gas-line installation require only a utility-company approval?
No. Newton has its own gas-permit process through ISD. Other City and state approvals may also apply depending on excavation and project scope. Source: Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical Permits ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Whether the project is a new service, an extension, or a replacement
  • Which gas utility must coordinate the service work
  • Required gas permit documents and license information
  • Whether trenching, street opening, or dewatering permits are required
  • Pressure-test and inspection requirements
  • Whether the project needs a separate electrical or building permit

Contact: Inspectional Services Department ↗
