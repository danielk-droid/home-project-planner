# What permits do I need to remodel a kitchen in Newton, MA?

> A kitchen remodel that changes the building or regulated equipment may require a building permit. Electrical, plumbing, and gas work are handled through separate Newton trade-permit applications. The exact permit list depends on whether the project changes wiring, plumbing, gas piping, walls, or the room layout.

## Building permit review

- The state building code requires an application and permit before regulated construction or alteration work begins. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## Separate trade permits

- Newton ISD provides separate plumbing, gas, and electrical permit applications and requires the appropriate signed license documentation for those applications. (Source: [Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/plumbing-gas-and-electrical))

## Questions and answers

### Does replacing kitchen cabinets automatically require every trade permit?

No universal answer is established by the cited sources. The trade permits depend on whether the work includes regulated electrical, plumbing, or gas installations or alterations. (Source: [Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/plumbing-gas-and-electrical))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the layout, walls, or structural members will change
- Whether new or relocated receptacles, circuits, or appliances require an electrical permit
- Whether plumbing or gas piping will be installed or altered
- Whether ventilation, fire-safety, or energy-code requirements apply
- Required inspection stages and approved plans
- Contact: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services)
