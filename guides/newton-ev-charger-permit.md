# Do I need an electrical permit to install an EV home charger in Newton, MA?

> Installing a home EV charger involves electrical work that should be reviewed through Newton ISD's electrical permit process. Newton provides a separate electrical application and requires the appropriate signed license documentation. Confirm the exact permit and inspection requirements for the charger's voltage, circuit, and installation location.

## Electrical permit process

- Newton ISD administers electrical permits through its trade-permit process and lists signed license documentation among the required application materials. (Source: [Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/plumbing-gas-and-electrical))

## Inspection

- Newton requires a final inspection for all permits. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))

## Questions and answers

### Does Newton publish a separate EV-charger permit threshold?

The sources reviewed do not establish a separate EV-charger threshold. Confirm the electrical permit application and inspection requirements with Newton ISD. (Source: [Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/plumbing-gas-and-electrical))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether a dedicated circuit or panel work is needed
- Whether the charger is indoors or outdoors
- Whether the electrical service has enough capacity
- Whether trenching or exterior site work triggers separate Engineering or Dig Safe requirements
- The applicable inspection and final approval steps
- Contact: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services)
