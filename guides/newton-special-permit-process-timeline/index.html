How long does a City Council or ZBA special permit take in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 2 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

Massachusetts law generally requires a special-permit public hearing within 65 days after the application is filed and a decision within 90 days after the public hearing. The time limits may be extended by written agreement. Newton's City Council is the special-permit granting authority for the City's zoning process, and the full project timeline may be longer because application preparation and review occur before the hearing.

Official source: https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleVII/Chapter40A/Section9 ↗ Newton Special Permits and Land Use ↗

State hearing deadline

  • Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 40A, Section 9 sets a general 65-day deadline for the special-permit public hearing after filing. (https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLa ↗)

State decision deadline

  • The statute sets a general 90-day period for the decision after the public hearing, subject to a written extension agreement. (https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLa ↗)

Newton's review process

  • Newton's special-permit process involves an application to the City Council and a public hearing before the Land Use Committee. (Newton Special Permits and Land Use ↗)

Questions and answers

Does the 65-day deadline mean a Newton special permit is always finished within 65 days?
No. The 65-day period is the general statutory deadline for holding the public hearing after filing. The decision period follows the hearing, and written extensions may be agreed. Source: https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleVII/Chapter40A/Section9 ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Whether the application is complete and accepted for filing
  • The current Land Use Committee meeting calendar
  • Whether a written extension agreement is anticipated
  • Whether the project requires a separate site-plan or other permit
  • The decision filing date and appeal deadline
