CARPORTS

Can I build a carport in Newton, MA, and what permits are required?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 3 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

A new permanent carport should be reviewed by Newton ISD as a building or structure project and checked against the property's accessory-building or principal-building zoning rules. Newton's accessory-building setbacks generally include 5 feet from side and rear lot lines and 5 feet from the principal building. The exact treatment depends on the carport's design and location.

Official source: Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2 ↗ Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3 ↗

Building permit review

  • The state building code requires a permit before regulated construction of a building or structure. (Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 C ↗)

Accessory-building location

  • Newton's accessory-building ordinance generally requires setbacks from side and rear lot lines and separation from the principal building. (Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Sec ↗)

Questions and answers

Is an open-sided carport automatically exempt from building and zoning rules?
The cited sources do not establish a general carport exemption. Confirm how Newton classifies the proposed permanent structure and which permits apply. Source: Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2 ↗, Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3 ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Whether the carport is classified as an accessory building or part of the principal building
  • Required height, setbacks, and maximum footprint
  • Structural design requirements for wind and snow loads
  • Driveway and parking layout compliance
  • Whether zoning relief or a special permit is required

Contact: Planning & Development Department ↗
