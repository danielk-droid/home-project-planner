# What are the zoning setbacks in Newton, MA?

> Newton setbacks depend on your district and on whether your lot was created before or after December 7, 1953. For newer lots, the front, side and rear setbacks are 40, 20 and 25 feet in SR1, 30, 15 and 15 feet in SR2, and 30, 10 and 15 feet in SR3 (Ord. Sec. 3.1.3).

## Single Residence (Sec. 3.1.3)

- Lots created on or after December 7, 1953: front SR1 40', SR2 30', SR3 30'; side SR1 20', SR2 15', SR3 10'; rear SR1 25', SR2 15', SR3 15'. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- Lots created before December 7, 1953: front 25' in all SR districts; side SR1 12.5', SR2 7.5', SR3 7.5'; rear SR1 25', SR2 15', SR3 15'. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

## Multi-Residence (Sec. 3.2.3)

- Lots created on or after December 7, 1953: front MR1 30', MR2 25', MR3 15', MR4 15'; side MR1 10', MR2 10', MR3 7.5', MR4 10'; rear 15' in all MR districts. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- Lots created before December 7, 1953: front MR1 25', MR2 25', MR3 15'; side 7.5' in MR1–MR3; rear 15' in MR1–MR3. The table gives no MR4 values for these older lots. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

## Front setback averaging

- Averaging applies only to the front setback, and no part of a residential building may be closer than 10 feet to the street line (Sec. 1.5.3.B). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

## Questions and answers

### What is the side setback in SR2?

15 feet for lots created on or after December 7, 1953, and 7.5 feet for older lots (Sec. 3.1.3). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### Why does the lot creation date matter?

Sec. 3.1.3 and 3.2.3 give different setbacks for lots created before December 7, 1953. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### Can front setback averaging reduce my front setback?

Averaging applies only to the front setback, and no part of a residential building may be closer than 10 feet to the street line (Sec. 1.5.3.B). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### What is the rear setback in SR1?

25 feet for lots of either date (Sec. 3.1.3). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

## What to confirm with the City

- When your lot was created.
- Whether front setback averaging applies to your lot.
- Setbacks for accessory buildings, which this guide does not cover.
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
