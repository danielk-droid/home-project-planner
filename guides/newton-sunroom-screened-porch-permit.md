# Do I need a permit for a screened porch or three-season sunroom in Newton, MA?

> A new screened porch or sunroom is a building project that should be reviewed by Newton ISD before construction. The structure must also meet the property's zoning standards for setbacks, height, and lot coverage. The exact foundation, frost-depth, and enclosure requirements depend on the design and applicable code.

## Building permit review

- The state building code requires a permit before regulated construction of a building or structure. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## Site and zoning review

- Newton Engineering reviews certain site plans involving additions, grading, impervious-area changes, and other site work. (Source: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))

## Questions and answers

### Is a screened porch treated the same as an open patio?

Do not assume so. A porch or sunroom is a structure, and the building and zoning requirements depend on its foundation, enclosure, location, and design. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download); [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the porch is open, screened, three-season, or conditioned living space
- Applicable foundation and frost-protection requirements
- Setbacks, lot coverage, and floor-area treatment
- Whether the structure changes the home's height or nonconforming status
- Historic, wetlands, tree, and stormwater review
- Contact: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services)
