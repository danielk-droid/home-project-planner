# Do I need a permit to replace siding in Newton, MA?

> The state building code exempts ordinary repairs, but the exact permit requirement for full siding replacement depends on the work being performed. Newton has a roofing and siding permit application category. Properties in a Local Historic District must obtain historic review for exterior changes, including siding replacement.

## Ordinary siding repairs

- The state building code exempts ordinary repairs from its permit application requirement. It does not define every possible siding replacement project as an ordinary repair. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## Historic property review

- Newton's historic preservation guidance says even small repairs and replacements such as siding may require review by preservation staff or the district commission. (Source: [Newton Historical Commission](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/historic-preservation/newton-historical-commission))

## Questions and answers

### Can I replace siding without historic review if I use the same material?

Do not assume so. Newton says small siding repairs and replacements in Local Historic Districts require preservation review; staff can determine whether the work qualifies for an administrative approval. (Source: [Newton Historical Commission](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/historic-preservation/newton-historical-commission))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the work is a minor repair or full re-siding
- Whether sheathing, insulation, or weather barriers will be changed
- Whether a Newton building permit is required for the specific scope
- Whether the property is in a Local Historic District, is a Landmark, or has a Preservation Restriction
- Contact: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services)
