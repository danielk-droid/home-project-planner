ZONING

What are Newton's SR and MR zoning districts?

SHORT ANSWER

Newton's ordinance sets up Single Residence 1, 2 and 3 districts and Multi-Residence 1 through 4 districts. Detached single-family homes are permitted in all of them. Detached two-family homes are permitted in MR1–MR4 but not in SR1–SR3.

Official source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30 ↗

What the use table says (Sec. 3.4.1)

  • The ordinance establishes Single Residence 1, 2 and 3 and Multi-Residence 1, 2, 3 and 4 districts (Sec. 1.3.1). (Zoning Ord. ↗)
  • Detached single-family housing: permitted in SR1–SR3 and MR1–MR4. (Zoning Ord. ↗)
  • Detached two-family housing: not allowed in SR1–SR3; permitted in MR1–MR4. (Zoning Ord. ↗)
  • Multi-family dwellings: not allowed in SR1–SR3 or MR1; special permit in MR2–MR4. (Zoning Ord. ↗)

Questions and answers

Can I build a two-family house in an SR district?
The use table marks detached two-family housing as not allowed in SR1–SR3. Source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30 ↗
Where are multi-family buildings allowed?
By special permit in MR2–MR4. They are not allowed in SR1–SR3 or MR1. Source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30 ↗
Do setbacks differ by district?
Yes. Sec. 3.1.3 and 3.2.3 set different setbacks by district and by lot creation date. Source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30 ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Your exact district and any overlay districts on your lot.
