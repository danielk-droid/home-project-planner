# What can you build on a pre-existing nonconforming lot or home in Newton, MA?

> An existing nonconforming lot or structure does not automatically make every future addition or alteration permissible. Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 40A, Section 6 and Newton Zoning Ordinance Section 7.8 govern nonconforming situations. The result depends on the property's legal history and the proposed change, so obtain a parcel-specific determination before designing the project.

## Questions and answers

### Can I enlarge a home that already violates a setback?

Do not assume the existing condition allows an addition. Newton must evaluate the proposed change under state law and its local nonconforming-structure rules. (Source: [Massachusetts General Laws, Chapter 40A, Section 6](https://malegislature.gov/laws/generallaws/parti/titlevii/chapter40a/section6); [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638055970231470000))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the lot or structure was lawfully created and when
- Which dimensional requirements are currently nonconforming
- Whether the proposed work increases the nonconformity
- Whether a finding, special permit, or variance is required
- Whether prior permits, decisions, or recorded plans affect the property
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
