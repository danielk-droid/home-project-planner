# What are the tree replacement and mitigation payment rules in Newton, MA?

> Newton's Tree Preservation Ordinance uses replacement inches based on the diameter of the removed tree. The City allows an applicant to plant replacement trees, make a payment in lieu of planting, or use a combination. The exact payment amount must be confirmed from the current City schedule; it should not be estimated from replacement inches alone.

## Replacement-inch calculation

- Newton's replacement table bases the required replacement inches on the size of the removed tree. Its examples show that a 14-inch tree requires 14 replacement inches and a 26-inch tree requires 39 replacement inches. (Source: [Newton Tree Preservation Ordinance Replacement and Payment Table, 2024](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/117200/638445489487430000))

## Payment instead of planting

- Newton allows an applicant to make a payment to the City in lieu of planting replacement trees, or to combine planting and payment. (Source: [Newton Tree Preservation Ordinance Replacement and Payment Table, 2024](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/117200/638445489487430000))

## Questions and answers

### Is the replacement requirement always one inch planted for each inch removed?

No. Newton's replacement table increases the required replacement inches for larger trees, so the calculation is not always one-for-one. (Source: [Newton Tree Preservation Ordinance Replacement and Payment Table, 2024](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/117200/638445489487430000))

## What to confirm with the City

- The current replacement-inch calculation for the specific tree size
- The current payment-per-inch rate and payment destination
- Whether the City requires planting, payment, or a combination
- The permitted tree species, size, and planting location
- Whether a tree warden determination is required for the tree
- Contact: [Newton Urban Forestry / Tree Warden](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/parks-recreation-culture/urban-forestry)
