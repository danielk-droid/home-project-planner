# Do I need a building permit to remove an interior wall in Newton, MA?

> Removing or changing a load-bearing wall requires building-code review and a building permit when the work removes or changes structural support. The Massachusetts code specifically identifies removal or cutting of a structural beam or load-bearing support as work requiring a permit. Newton ISD should review the project plans before construction.

## Structural permit requirement

- The state code identifies substantial cutting of a wall or partition and removal or cutting of structural beams or load-bearing supports as work requiring a permit. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## Plans and inspections

- Newton ISD enforces the state building code and issues permits for regulated construction and alteration work. (Source: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services))

## Questions and answers

### Can I remove a wall without checking whether it is load-bearing?

Do not assume a wall is non-structural. Have the existing framing and proposed work evaluated and confirm permit requirements with Newton ISD before starting. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the wall carries roof, floor, or other structural loads
- Whether an architect or structural engineer must prepare plans
- Whether temporary support, a beam, or new posts are part of the design
- Required inspections of structural connections and supports
- Whether the work also changes electrical, plumbing, gas, or fire-safety systems
- Contact: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services)
