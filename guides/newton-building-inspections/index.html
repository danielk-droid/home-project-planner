How do building inspections work in Newton, MA?

SHORT ANSWER

The permit holder arranges every inspection. For building inspections, call before 8 AM on the day the work is ready, and the inspector calls back by 9:30 AM to schedule it. Electrical and plumbing/gas inspections are scheduled in advance, and the final building inspection comes last.

Official source: Inspection Requests ↗

Scheduling

  • The permit holder must arrange all inspections. (Inspection requests ↗)
  • Building inspections are same-day when the job is complete and ready. (Inspection requests ↗)
  • Call before 8 AM on the desired day, and leave a voicemail if no one answers. The inspector calls back by 9:30 AM to schedule. (Inspection requests ↗)
  • Electrical and plumbing/gas inspections are scheduled in advance. (Inspection requests ↗)

What must be ready at each stage

  • Excavation: inspected at the bottom of the hole, before footings or crushed stone. (Inspection requests ↗)
  • Footings: forms stripped before the wall is poured. Deck footings are inspected before pouring. (Inspection requests ↗)
  • Before backfill: foundation poured and coated if required. An as-built may be requested. (Inspection requests ↗)
  • Rough: building weather-tight with roof, windows and doors installed and siding 75% complete. Rough plumbing, electrical and HVAC inspections done first, and all firestopping complete. (Inspection requests ↗)
  • Insulation: insulation and vapor barrier installed, and a mid-point HERS rating uploaded to the portal. (Inspection requests ↗)
  • Final: last, after all other inspections pass. (Inspection requests ↗)

Questions and answers

How do I schedule a building inspection in Newton?
Call before 8 AM on the day you want it. Leave a voicemail if no one answers, and the inspector calls back by 9:30 AM. Source: Inspection Requests ↗
Who is responsible for scheduling?
The permit holder. Source: Inspection Requests ↗
What is needed for a rough inspection?
A weather-tight building with roof, windows and doors installed, siding 75% complete, rough trade inspections passed, and firestopping complete. Source: Inspection Requests ↗
What is needed for an insulation inspection?
Completed insulation, an installed vapor barrier, and a mid-point HERS rating uploaded to the portal. Source: Inspection Requests ↗
When is the final inspection?
Last, after all other inspections pass. Source: Inspection Requests ↗
