# Do I need a permit to place a construction dumpster or storage container on the street in Newton, MA?

> The official sources reviewed do not establish one complete Newton permit rule for every dumpster or storage container. A container placed in a public street, sidewalk, or other public right-of-way should be reviewed with the City before delivery. Placement on private property should also be checked for zoning, access, and site restrictions.

## Public-way placement

- Newton Engineering reviews work and site conditions involving the public right-of-way. (Source: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))

## Construction permits

- Newton ISD and other City departments administer separate permits for work under their respective authority. (Source: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services))

## Questions and answers

### Can I place a dumpster on the street without contacting the City?

Do not assume that public-way placement is allowed without approval. Confirm the current street-occupancy or right-of-way process with Newton before delivery. (Source: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the container will be entirely on private property or in the public way
- Which City department issues the applicable street-occupancy approval
- Required reflective markers, barricades, or traffic controls
- Allowed placement period and renewal process
- Whether a sidewalk or street closure requires additional approval
- Whether the private driveway placement complies with site restrictions
- Contact: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering)
