STREET OPENINGS

When do you need a street opening or trench permit from Newton Engineering?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 2 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

Work that opens a Newton street or public right-of-way should be reviewed by Newton Engineering before excavation begins. Massachusetts also regulates construction-related trenches and requires compliance with state trench-safety rules. The exact Newton application, insurance, bond, and restoration requirements depend on the type and location of work.

Official source: Newton Engineering Division ↗ Massachusetts Trenches Laws and Regulations ↗

Newton Engineering review

  • Newton Engineering reviews site work and public-way projects, including utility and street-related work. (Newton Engineering Division ↗)

Massachusetts trench safety

  • Massachusetts trench regulations allow permitting authorities to stop trench work when required permits or public-safety protections are missing. (Massachusetts Trenches Laws and Regulati ↗)

Questions and answers

Does a Newton Engineering permit replace state trench requirements?
No. A local street or Engineering permit does not replace applicable Massachusetts trench-safety requirements. Source: Massachusetts Trenches Laws and Regulations ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Whether the excavation is in a public street, sidewalk, or private property
  • The correct Newton Engineering or street-opening application
  • Current bond, insurance, and restoration requirements
  • Whether a separate state trench permit is required
  • Required inspection and closeout documents
  • Dig Safe notification requirements
