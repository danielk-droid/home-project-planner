ELECTRICAL PANELS

What permits are required to upgrade an electrical panel in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 3 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

An electrical panel upgrade should be handled through Newton ISD's electrical permit process. Newton provides a separate electrical application and requires appropriate signed license documentation. The available sources do not establish one universal utility-coordination sequence for every service upgrade.

Official source: Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical Permits ↗ Inspection Requests ↗

Electrical permit application

  • Newton ISD administers electrical permits and requires the appropriate signed license documentation for trade-permit applications. (Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical ↗)

Inspections

  • Newton states that a final inspection is required for all permits. (Inspection requests ↗)

Questions and answers

Does a 100-amp-to-200-amp upgrade have the same process as every other panel change?
The cited sources do not establish one process for every service size. Confirm the service rating, utility requirements, application documents, and inspections with Newton ISD and the electric utility. Source: Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical Permits ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Whether the service size is changing
  • Whether the utility must disconnect, reconnect, or approve the service
  • Whether the meter, service entrance, grounding, or feeder work changes
  • Whether other equipment changes require separate permits
  • The rough, service, and final inspection sequence

Contact: Inspectional Services Department ↗
