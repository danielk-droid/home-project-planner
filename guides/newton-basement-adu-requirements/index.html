BASEMENT ADUs

What building code rules apply to building an ADU in a basement in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 2 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

A basement ADU must qualify as a self-contained dwelling unit with sleeping, cooking, and sanitary facilities and a separate entrance that meets safe-egress requirements. Massachusetts ADU regulations do not replace the state building code or fire-safety rules. The exact ceiling height, emergency escape opening, and fire-separation requirements must be confirmed for the proposed basement design.

Official source: 760 CMR 71.00 — Protected Use ADUs ↗ Inspectional Services Department ↗

State ADU definition

  • Massachusetts defines an ADU as a self-contained housing unit with sleeping, cooking, and sanitary facilities on the same lot as the principal dwelling, with a separate entrance. (760 CMR 71 ↗)

Building and life-safety code

  • The state ADU regulation states that it is not intended to supersede the Building Code, Fire Code, Lead Law, or other state and federal health and safety laws. (760 CMR 71 ↗)

Questions and answers

Does the ADU law waive basement egress or ceiling-height rules?
No. The ADU regulation does not supersede the state Building Code or Fire Code. The proposed basement unit must meet the applicable building and life-safety requirements. Source: 760 CMR 71.00 — Protected Use ADUs ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Minimum ceiling height for the proposed basement layout
  • Required emergency escape and rescue openings
  • Separate entrance and safe-egress design
  • Fire-resistance, sound separation, smoke alarm, and carbon-monoxide requirements
  • Moisture, ventilation, insulation, and combustion-air requirements
  • The applicable Newton ADU size and zoning limits

Contact: Inspectional Services Department ↗
