TREE REPLACEMENT

What are the tree replacement and mitigation payment rules in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 3 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

Newton's Tree Preservation Ordinance uses replacement inches based on the diameter of the removed tree. The City allows an applicant to plant replacement trees, make a payment in lieu of planting, or use a combination. The exact payment amount must be confirmed from the current City schedule; it should not be estimated from replacement inches alone.

Official source: Newton Tree Preservation Ordinance Replacement and Payment Table, 2024 ↗ Tree Preservation Ordinance ↗

Replacement-inch calculation

  • Newton's replacement table bases the required replacement inches on the size of the removed tree. Its examples show that a 14-inch tree requires 14 replacement inches and a 26-inch tree requires 39 replacement inches. (Newton Tree Preservation Ordinance Repla ↗)

Payment instead of planting

  • Newton allows an applicant to make a payment to the City in lieu of planting replacement trees, or to combine planting and payment. (Newton Tree Preservation Ordinance Repla ↗)

Questions and answers

Is the replacement requirement always one inch planted for each inch removed?
No. Newton's replacement table increases the required replacement inches for larger trees, so the calculation is not always one-for-one. Source: Newton Tree Preservation Ordinance Replacement and Payment Table, 2024 ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • The current replacement-inch calculation for the specific tree size
  • The current payment-per-inch rate and payment destination
  • Whether the City requires planting, payment, or a combination
  • The permitted tree species, size, and planting location
  • Whether a tree warden determination is required for the tree

Contact: Newton Urban Forestry / Tree Warden ↗
