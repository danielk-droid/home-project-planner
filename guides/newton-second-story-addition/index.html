SECOND STORIES

What zoning and building code rules apply to adding a second story in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 4 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

A second-story addition requires building-code review and a building permit. Newton zoning controls the height, setbacks, and floor-area ratio of the expanded principal building. Whether the design is allowed by right or needs a special permit depends on the property's zoning district and existing conditions.

Official source: Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2 ↗ Newton Special Permits and Land Use ↗

Building and structural review

  • The state building code requires a permit before regulated construction or alteration of a building. (Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 C ↗)

Zoning standards

  • Newton's special-permit process covers projects that need approval under the zoning ordinance, including certain additions and nonconforming structures. (Newton Special Permits and Land Use ↗)

Questions and answers

Can every Newton home add a second story within a 36-foot height limit?
No citywide answer is established by that single number. Height, story count, roof form, and floor-area limits depend on the property's district and applicable zoning provisions. Source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3 ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • The zoning district and applicable principal-building height limit
  • Existing and proposed floor-area ratio
  • Required front, side, and rear setbacks
  • Whether the existing foundation and framing can support the addition
  • Whether special-permit or nonconforming-structure review applies
  • Required architect, engineer, and building permit documents

Contact: Planning & Development Department ↗
