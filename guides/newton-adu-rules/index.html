What are the ADU rules in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 2 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

Newton allows one ADU per lot on a single- or two-family property, in any zoning district. No extra parking is required, the ADU can't be sold separately, and rentals must be at least 30 days. The baseline size is 1,000 sq ft or 50% of the home's floor area, whichever is less, and a special permit can allow more.

Official source: Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU) ↗ Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30 ↗

Core rules (Ord. Sec. 6.7.1)

  • A single- or two-family home may add one ADU regardless of zoning district, according to Newton Planning. (Newton ADU page ↗)
  • No more than one ADU per lot, and no separate ownership (Sec. 6.7.1.C.1–2). (Zoning Ord. ↗)
  • No additional parking is required (Sec. 6.7.1.C.5). (Zoning Ord. ↗)
  • Minimum rental or occupancy term of 30 days for either dwelling (Sec. 6.7.1.C.4). (Zoning Ord. ↗)

Size

  • Internal ADU: up to 1,000 sq ft or 50% of the total gross floor area, whichever is less. The total counts both units (Sec. 6.7.1.D.2). (Zoning Ord. ↗)
  • Internal ADU by special permit: up to 1,200 sq ft (Sec. 6.7.1.D.2.b). (Zoning Ord. ↗)
  • Newton Planning describes a conditional by-right allowance for internal ADUs of up to the smaller of 1,200 sq ft or 50%, for a residence whose certificate of occupancy is at least four years old. (Newton ADU page ↗)
  • Detached ADU: up to 1,000 sq ft or 50%, whichever is less, or up to 1,500 sq ft by special permit (Sec. 6.7.1.E.2). (Zoning Ord. ↗)

Questions and answers

Can I build an ADU in Newton?
Newton Planning says a single- or two-family home may add one ADU regardless of zoning district. Source: Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU) ↗
Do I need extra parking for a Newton ADU?
No. Sec. 6.7.1.C.5 requires no additional parking. Source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30 ↗
Can I Airbnb my ADU in Newton?
The ordinance sets a minimum rental or occupancy term of 30 days (Sec. 6.7.1.C.4). Source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30 ↗
How big can a detached ADU be in Newton?
1,000 sq ft or 50% of the total gross floor area, whichever is less, or up to 1,500 sq ft by special permit. Source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30 ↗
Can I sell the ADU separately?
No. The ordinance prohibits separate ownership (Sec. 6.7.1.C.1). Source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30 ↗
Who answers ADU questions in Newton?
Newton Planning lists an ADU questions email (adu@newtonma.gov) on its ADU page. Source: Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU) ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Whether your house meets the four-year certificate-of-occupancy condition.
  • Setbacks and design rules for detached ADUs.
