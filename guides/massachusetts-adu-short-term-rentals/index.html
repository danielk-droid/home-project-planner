ADU RENTALS

Can an ADU be used for Airbnb or short-term rentals in Massachusetts?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 3 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

Massachusetts state law allows municipalities to impose restrictions or prohibitions on short-term rentals of ADUs. Newton says its ADUs cannot be used as short-term rentals and must be rented for a period of more than 30 days. Confirm whether any separate rental registration or other housing requirements apply.

Official source: Massachusetts General Laws, Chapter 40A, Section 3 ↗ Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU) ↗

State authority

  • Massachusetts law allows local restrictions and prohibitions on short-term rentals of ADUs. (Massachusetts General Laws, Chapter 40A, ↗)

Newton's local rule

  • Newton states that ADUs are prohibited from being used as short-term rentals and must be rented for more than 30 days. (Newton ADU page ↗)
