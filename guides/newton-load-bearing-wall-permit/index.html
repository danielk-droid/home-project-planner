STRUCTURAL WALLS

Do I need a building permit to remove an interior wall in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 2 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

Removing or changing a load-bearing wall requires building-code review and a building permit when the work removes or changes structural support. The Massachusetts code specifically identifies removal or cutting of a structural beam or load-bearing support as work requiring a permit. Newton ISD should review the project plans before construction.

Official source: Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2 ↗ Inspectional Services Department ↗

Structural permit requirement

  • The state code identifies substantial cutting of a wall or partition and removal or cutting of structural beams or load-bearing supports as work requiring a permit. (Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 C ↗)

Plans and inspections

  • Newton ISD enforces the state building code and issues permits for regulated construction and alteration work. (City ISD ↗)

Questions and answers

Can I remove a wall without checking whether it is load-bearing?
Do not assume a wall is non-structural. Have the existing framing and proposed work evaluated and confirm permit requirements with Newton ISD before starting. Source: Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2 ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Whether the wall carries roof, floor, or other structural loads
  • Whether an architect or structural engineer must prepare plans
  • Whether temporary support, a beam, or new posts are part of the design
  • Required inspections of structural connections and supports
  • Whether the work also changes electrical, plumbing, gas, or fire-safety systems

Contact: Inspectional Services Department ↗
