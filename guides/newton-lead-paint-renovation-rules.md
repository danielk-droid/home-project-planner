# What lead paint containment rules apply when renovating an older Newton home?

> Massachusetts requires lead-safe renovation work by a licensed lead-safe renovation contractor when paid work in a pre-1978 home disturbs more than 6 square feet of painted surface per room indoors or more than 20 square feet outdoors. A lead-safe renovation project is different from a deleading project. Confirm which rules apply before disturbing painted surfaces.

## When the lead-safe renovation rule applies

- Massachusetts applies its lead-safe renovation requirements to paid work in pre-1978 housing when more than 6 square feet of painted surface per room is disturbed indoors or more than 20 square feet is disturbed outdoors. (Source: [Massachusetts Lead-Safe Renovation for Homeowners](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/lead-safe-renovation-for-homeowners))

## Renovation versus deleading

- Massachusetts distinguishes renovation work from work intended to abate lead hazards or obtain a Letter of Compliance. (Source: [Massachusetts Lead-Safe Renovation for Homeowners](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/lead-safe-renovation-for-homeowners))

## Questions and answers

### Does every repair in a pre-1978 home require a deleading project?

No. Massachusetts distinguishes lead-safe renovation work from deleading. The applicable requirement depends on the work's purpose and the amount of painted surface disturbed. (Source: [Massachusetts Lead-Safe Renovation for Homeowners](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/lead-safe-renovation-for-homeowners))

## What to confirm with the City

- The home's construction year
- Whether painted surfaces contain lead or require testing
- The interior or exterior area of painted surface to be disturbed
- Whether the project is renovation work or deleading
- Whether a licensed lead-safe renovation supervisor must be on site
- Whether historic-property restrictions affect component removal or replacement
- Contact: [Massachusetts Lead-Safe Renovation for Homeowners](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/lead-safe-renovation-for-homeowners)
