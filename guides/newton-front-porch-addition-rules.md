# Can I build or enclose a front porch in Newton, MA under zoning setback rules?

> A new or enclosed front porch is a building project that should be checked against Newton's principal-building setbacks and building-code requirements. Whether the porch may extend into a required front setback depends on the property's zoning district and the exact design. Projects outside by-right zoning standards may need City Council approval.

## Building permit

- The state building code requires a permit before regulated construction or alteration of a building. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## Zoning approval

- Newton describes by-right projects as those that meet the dimensional standards for the zoning district and use rules. Projects that require zoning relief may need a special permit or variance. (Source: [Newton Special Permits and Land Use](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/development-review/special-permits-land-use))

## Questions and answers

### Can an open porch automatically extend into the required front setback?

The sources reviewed do not establish a universal porch exception. Confirm the porch type, location, and applicable district rule with Newton Planning. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638055970231470000))

## What to confirm with the City

- The required front setback for the property
- Whether the porch is open, screened, or enclosed
- Whether the porch is counted in floor area or lot coverage
- Whether a special permit or variance is required
- Historic review and building-code requirements
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
