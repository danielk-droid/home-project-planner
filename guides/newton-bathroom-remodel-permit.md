# What permits do I need to remodel a bathroom in Newton, MA?

> A bathroom remodel may require a building permit for regulated construction or alteration work, plus separate plumbing, gas, or electrical permits when those systems are changed. Newton ISD handles the trade-permit applications. Replacing a fixture without changing a regulated system should be confirmed with ISD rather than assumed to be permit-free.

## Building and trade permits

- The state building code requires a permit before regulated construction, alteration, repair, or installation of regulated equipment. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))
- Newton handles plumbing, gas, and electrical permits through its ISD trade-permit process. (Source: [Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/plumbing-gas-and-electrical))

## Inspections

- Newton states that a final inspection is required for all permits. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))

## Questions and answers

### Does changing a bathroom vanity always require a plumbing permit?

The sources reviewed do not establish a universal fixture-replacement exemption. Ask Newton ISD whether the exact replacement changes plumbing work that requires a permit. (Source: [Newton ISD Plumbing, Gas and Electrical Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/plumbing-gas-and-electrical))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether plumbing fixtures or piping will be relocated or altered
- Whether new electrical circuits, receptacles, or ventilation equipment will be installed
- Whether walls, floors, or structural components will be changed
- Applicable bathroom ventilation, electrical safety, and waterproofing code details
- Required rough and final inspections
- Contact: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services)
