# What are the specific rules and setbacks for building a detached ADU in Newton, MA?

> Newton allows a new detached ADU by right up to 1,000 square feet or 50% of the principal dwelling's total gross floor area, whichever is smaller. Certain existing accessory structures and owner-occupancy situations may qualify for a detached ADU up to 1,200 square feet, while a detached ADU up to 1,500 square feet requires a special permit. Setbacks and height depend on the local ordinance and building design.

## By-right size

- Newton's standard by-right detached ADU limit is 1,000 square feet or 50% of the principal dwelling's total gross floor area, whichever is smaller. (Source: [Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/zoning-and-development/zoning-updates/accessory-dwelling-units-adu))

## Larger detached ADUs

- Newton identifies qualifying detached ADUs up to 1,200 square feet that may be permitted by right under stated conditions, and detached ADUs up to 1,500 square feet that require a special permit. (Source: [Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/zoning-and-development/zoning-updates/accessory-dwelling-units-adu); [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 6.7](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638887019852292948))

## Questions and answers

### Can every detached ADU be 1,200 square feet by right?

No. Newton lists conditions for the 1,200-square-foot path, including conditions involving an existing accessory structure or owner occupancy. The standard new detached ADU limit is lower. (Source: [Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/zoning-and-development/zoning-updates/accessory-dwelling-units-adu))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the detached unit is new construction or uses an existing lawful accessory structure
- Whether the principal dwelling or accessory structure meets the four-year condition
- The applicable height and side, rear, and front setbacks
- Whether the proposed unit requires a special permit
- Whether the property is a Local Landmark or in a Local Historic District
- Required building, utility, fire, and site approvals
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
