# What are Newton's SR and MR zoning districts?

> Newton's ordinance sets up Single Residence 1, 2 and 3 districts and Multi-Residence 1 through 4 districts. Detached single-family homes are permitted in all of them. Detached two-family homes are permitted in MR1–MR4 but not in SR1–SR3.

## What the use table says (Sec. 3.4.1)

- The ordinance establishes Single Residence 1, 2 and 3 and Multi-Residence 1, 2, 3 and 4 districts (Sec. 1.3.1). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- Detached single-family housing: permitted in SR1–SR3 and MR1–MR4. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- Detached two-family housing: not allowed in SR1–SR3; permitted in MR1–MR4. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- Multi-family dwellings: not allowed in SR1–SR3 or MR1; special permit in MR2–MR4. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

## Questions and answers

### Can I build a two-family house in an SR district?

The use table marks detached two-family housing as not allowed in SR1–SR3. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### Where are multi-family buildings allowed?

By special permit in MR2–MR4. They are not allowed in SR1–SR3 or MR1. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### Do setbacks differ by district?

Yes. Sec. 3.1.3 and 3.2.3 set different setbacks by district and by lot creation date. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
