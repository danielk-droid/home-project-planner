# What does Massachusetts law say about municipal setbacks for ADUs?

> Massachusetts law protects a single ADU in a single-family residential zoning district from being prohibited or unreasonably restricted, but it expressly allows reasonable local rules for dimensional setbacks, bulk, height, and site-plan review. ADUs therefore do not receive a blanket exemption from local zoning dimensions. Newton's current ADU ordinance must be checked for the particular unit.

## What state law protects

- Massachusetts prohibits municipalities from unreasonably restricting or requiring discretionary zoning approval for the single protected ADU described in Chapter 40A, Section 3. (Source: [Massachusetts General Laws, Chapter 40A, Section 3](https://malegislature.gov/laws/generallaws/parti/titlevii/chapter40a/section3))

## What local rules may still regulate

- The statute permits reasonable regulations concerning dimensional setbacks, bulk, height, and site-plan review. (Source: [Massachusetts General Laws, Chapter 40A, Section 3](https://malegislature.gov/laws/generallaws/parti/titlevii/chapter40a/section3))
- Newton publishes local ADU size, height, and setback standards for detached units. (Source: [Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/zoning-and-development/zoning-updates/accessory-dwelling-units-adu))

## Questions and answers

### Does state ADU law eliminate Newton's setback rules?

No. State law expressly allows reasonable local dimensional setback, bulk, and height regulations. (Source: [Massachusetts General Laws, Chapter 40A, Section 3](https://malegislature.gov/laws/generallaws/parti/titlevii/chapter40a/section3))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the ADU qualifies for the state protected-use provision
- The Newton zoning district and the applicable ADU setback rule
- Whether the unit is internal, attached, or detached
- Whether historic, wetlands, floodplain, or other site rules apply
- Whether the property is subject to a prior ADU approval or transition provision
