# What is the maximum lot coverage in Newton, MA?

> For lots created on or after December 7, 1953, maximum lot coverage is 15% in SR1, 20% in SR2 and 30% in SR3. Older lots allow 20%, 30% and 30%. MR districts allow 30% for detached single- and two-family homes (Ord. Sec. 3.1.3 and 3.2.3).

## Maximum lot coverage

- SR1 / SR2 / SR3, lots created on or after 12/7/1953: 15% / 20% / 30%. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- SR1 / SR2 / SR3, lots created before 12/7/1953: 20% / 30% / 30%. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- MR1–MR4, detached single- or two-family, lots created on or after 12/7/1953: 30% in each district. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- MR1–MR3, lots created before 12/7/1953: 30%. The table gives no MR4 value for these older lots. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

## Questions and answers

### What is the lot coverage limit in SR2?

20% for lots created on or after December 7, 1953, and 30% for older lots (Sec. 3.1.3). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### Is lot coverage the same as FAR?

No. FAR is a separate limit: gross floor area of all buildings divided by lot area (Sec. 1.5.5.A.1). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### What is the limit in MR districts?

30% for detached single- and two-family homes (Sec. 3.2.3). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
