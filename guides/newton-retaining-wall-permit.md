# Do I need a permit for a retaining wall in Newton, MA?

> A retaining wall no higher than 4 feet, measured from the bottom of its footing to the top of the wall, may qualify for the state building-permit exemption if it does not support a surcharge or impound certain liquids. Newton zoning separately requires a special permit for a retaining wall 4 feet or higher, so a wall can trigger local zoning review even when it falls within the state building-code exemption.

## State building-code exemption

- The state code exempts qualifying retaining walls no higher than 4 feet when height is measured from the bottom of the footing to the top of the wall. The exemption does not apply to walls supporting a surcharge or impounding the specified classes of liquids. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## Newton zoning review

- Newton zoning requires a special permit for a retaining wall 4 feet or higher. The ordinance also addresses combined walls located within 25 feet of each other. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638055970231470000))
- Newton Engineering reviews site plans involving retaining walls and grade alterations. (Source: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))

## Questions and answers

### Can a 4-foot retaining wall need a Newton special permit even if the state building code exempts it from a building permit?

Yes. The state building-code exemption and Newton's local special-permit rule are separate requirements. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download); [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638055970231470000))

## What to confirm with the City

- How the wall height will be measured under each applicable rule
- Whether nearby walls must be measured as a combined wall
- Whether the wall supports a surcharge or affects drainage
- Whether Engineering requires a site plan or other technical information
- Whether wetlands, easements, or historic review apply
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
