Can a homeowner do electrical, plumbing or gas work in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 4 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

Newton's electrical and plumbing/gas permit submissions require an appropriately signed license. Massachusetts issues plumbing permits only to licensed plumbers. The state lists minor repairs, such as fixing a leaky faucet or clearing a blocked drain, as not needing a permit.

Official source: Electrical Permits ↗ Plumbing & Gas Permits ↗ Plumbers and Gas Fitters Consumer Fact Sheet (Mass.gov) ↗

Newton requirements

  • Electrical permit submissions require an appropriately signed electrical license. (Electrical permits ↗)
  • Electrical submissions need liability insurance and workers' compensation unless the applicant is a sole proprietor. (Electrical permits ↗)
  • Plumbing and gas submissions require an appropriately signed plumbing or gas license. (Plumbing & gas permits ↗)
  • Replacing a gas water heater requires both a plumbing permit and a gas permit. (Plumbing & gas permits ↗)

Massachusetts rules

  • Massachusetts issues plumbing permits only to licensed plumbers. (Mass.gov fact sheet ↗)
  • Only licensed professionals may install, remove or repair plumbing. (Mass.gov fact sheet ↗)
  • Fixing a leaky faucet, valve or other working part, or clearing a blocked drain, is a minor repair that does not need a permit. (Mass.gov fact sheet ↗)
  • Licensed master or journeyman plumbers may do gas fitting, but a gas-fitter license does not cover plumbing work. (Mass.gov fact sheet ↗)

Questions and answers

Can I pull my own plumbing permit in Massachusetts?
The state says plumbing permits are issued only to licensed plumbers. Source: Plumbers and Gas Fitters Consumer Fact Sheet (Mass.gov) ↗
Do I need a permit to fix a leaky faucet?
No. The state lists fixing a leaky faucet, valve or other working part, or clearing a blocked drain, as minor repairs that need no permit. Source: Plumbers and Gas Fitters Consumer Fact Sheet (Mass.gov) ↗
What permits does a gas water heater replacement need in Newton?
Both a plumbing permit and a gas permit. Source: Plumbing & Gas Permits ↗
What does a Newton electrical permit application need?
An appropriately signed electrical license, plus liability insurance and workers' compensation unless the applicant is a sole proprietor. Source: Electrical Permits ↗
Can a gas fitter do plumbing?
No. The state says a gas-fitter license does not authorize plumbing work. Source: Plumbers and Gas Fitters Consumer Fact Sheet (Mass.gov) ↗
