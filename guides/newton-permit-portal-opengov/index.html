PERMIT PROCESS

How do I apply for and track a permit on Newton's online portal?

SHORT ANSWER

Newton's Inspectional Services takes applications online through Apply for Permits, which requires an OpenGov account. The City also provides a public search by address or record number, which you can use to confirm permits are closed before you make the final payment to a contractor.

Official source: Inspectional Services Department ↗

Using the portal

  • Use the City's Apply for Permits link to start or manage an application. (City ISD ↗)
  • An OpenGov account is required for online applications. (City ISD ↗)
  • The City provides a public search link for address or record-number searches. (City ISD ↗)
  • Check the permitting website to confirm permits are closed before you pay the contractor in full. (Closing permits ↗)

Questions and answers

Do I need an account to apply?
Yes. The City says an OpenGov account is required for online applications. Source: Inspectional Services Department ↗
Can I look up permits on my address?
Yes. The City provides a public search by address or record number. Source: Inspectional Services Department ↗
When should I check my permit status?
The City advises checking that permits are closed before you make the final payment to a contractor. Source: How to Close Open Permits ↗
Who can I call for help?
Inspectional Services at (617) 796-1060, City Hall Room 202. Source: Inspectional Services — Contact Us ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Which application type matches your project.
