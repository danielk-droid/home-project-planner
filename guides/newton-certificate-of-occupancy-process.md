# When is a Certificate of Occupancy or Completion required in Newton, MA?

> Newton requires final inspections for permits and tells property owners to close electrical, plumbing, gas, and other department permits before requesting final inspection on a building permit. Whether a project needs a Certificate of Occupancy, Certificate of Completion, or another final sign-off depends on the project and approval conditions. Confirm the required certificate with ISD.

## Final inspections

- Newton states that a final inspection is required for all permits. (Source: [Inspection Requests](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/inspection-requests-2376))

## Close related permits

- Newton instructs property owners to close electrical, plumbing, gas, and other department permits before calling for a final inspection on the building permit. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))

## Questions and answers

### Can I consider a project complete when the contractor says the work is finished?

Not necessarily. Newton advises property owners to verify that required permits are closed and final inspections are complete before treating the project as closed out. (Source: [How to Close Open Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/how-to-close-open-permits))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the project requires a Certificate of Occupancy, Certificate of Completion, or another document
- Which building, electrical, plumbing, gas, fire, and Engineering approvals remain open
- Whether as-built plans or final site inspections are required
- Whether any historic or Conservation conditions require a separate sign-off
- Whether the City portal shows all permits as closed
- Contact: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services)
