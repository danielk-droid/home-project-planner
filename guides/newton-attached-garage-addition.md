# What rules apply to adding an attached garage in Newton, MA?

> An attached garage addition is an alteration or expansion of the principal building and should be reviewed by Newton ISD. The addition must also comply with the property's zoning standards, including setbacks and floor-area and lot-coverage limits. The exact fire-separation and structural details depend on the proposed plans and applicable building code.

## Building permit

- The state building code requires a permit before regulated construction or alteration of a building. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## Zoning relief

- Newton describes a project that meets its district dimensional standards and allowed uses as a by-right project; projects requiring zoning relief may need City Council approval. (Source: [Newton Special Permits and Land Use](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/development-review/special-permits-land-use))

## Questions and answers

### Does an attached garage use the detached accessory-building setback rule?

Do not apply the detached accessory-building rule automatically. An attached garage is part of the principal building, so confirm the principal-building setbacks for the property's zoning district. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638055970231470000))

## What to confirm with the City

- The front, side, and rear setbacks for the principal building
- The property's floor-area ratio and lot-coverage limits
- Required fire separation between the garage and dwelling
- Foundation, structural, and energy-code requirements
- Whether special-permit approval is needed
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
