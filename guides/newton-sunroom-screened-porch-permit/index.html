PORCHES AND SUNROOMS

Do I need a permit for a screened porch or three-season sunroom in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 3 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

A new screened porch or sunroom is a building project that should be reviewed by Newton ISD before construction. The structure must also meet the property's zoning standards for setbacks, height, and lot coverage. The exact foundation, frost-depth, and enclosure requirements depend on the design and applicable code.

Official source: Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2 ↗ Inspectional Services Department ↗

Building permit review

  • The state building code requires a permit before regulated construction of a building or structure. (Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 C ↗)

Site and zoning review

  • Newton Engineering reviews certain site plans involving additions, grading, impervious-area changes, and other site work. (Newton Engineering Division ↗)

Questions and answers

Is a screened porch treated the same as an open patio?
Do not assume so. A porch or sunroom is a structure, and the building and zoning requirements depend on its foundation, enclosure, location, and design. Source: Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2 ↗, Inspectional Services Department ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Whether the porch is open, screened, three-season, or conditioned living space
  • Applicable foundation and frost-protection requirements
  • Setbacks, lot coverage, and floor-area treatment
  • Whether the structure changes the home's height or nonconforming status
  • Historic, wetlands, tree, and stormwater review

Contact: Inspectional Services Department ↗
