# What is the difference between a special permit and a variance in Newton?

> The City Council grants special permits, by a two-thirds vote of all councilors, together with site plan approval. Variances are heard by the Zoning Board of Appeals. A variance requires conditions such as soil, shape or topography that especially affect the property, plus substantial hardship.

## Special permits

- The City Council grants special permits by a two-thirds vote of all the Council (Sec. 7.3.3.A). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- A special-permit application requires concurrent site plan approval (Sec. 7.3.1.A). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- Findings include an appropriate site, no adverse effect on the neighborhood, and no nuisance or serious vehicle or pedestrian hazard (Sec. 7.3.3.C). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- Examples include increased FAR and a third story for detached houses (Sec. 3.1.9.A.2, 3.2.11.A.2). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

## Variances

- Variance applications are heard by the Zoning Board of Appeals (Sec. 7.6.1.A). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- The property must have soil, shape or topography conditions that especially affect it, rather than the district generally (Sec. 7.6.1.B). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- Literal enforcement must cause substantial hardship (Sec. 7.6.1.C). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- Relief must not substantially harm the public good or undermine the ordinance's intent (Sec. 7.6.1.D). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

## Questions and answers

### Who approves special permits in Newton?

The City Council, by a two-thirds vote of all councilors (Sec. 7.3.3.A). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### Who hears variances?

The Zoning Board of Appeals (Sec. 7.6.1.A). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### Can I get a special permit to build a larger house?

The ordinance lists increased FAR as something a special permit may allow (Sec. 3.1.9.A.2). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### What do I need to show for a variance?

Conditions of soil, shape or topography that especially affect the property, substantial hardship, and no substantial detriment to the public good (Sec. 7.6.1). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
