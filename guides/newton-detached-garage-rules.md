# What are the zoning and permit rules for a detached garage in Newton, MA?

> A new detached garage is a building project that should be reviewed by Newton ISD and checked against the property's zoning district. Newton's accessory-building rules generally require at least 5 feet from side and rear lot lines and at least 5 feet from the principal building. Height, footprint, lot coverage, and any required zoning relief must be checked separately.

## Building permit

- The state building code generally requires a permit before constructing a regulated building or structure. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## Accessory-building setbacks

- Newton's accessory-building ordinance generally requires 5 feet from side and rear lot lines and 5 feet from the principal building on the same lot. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638055970231470000))

## Questions and answers

### Can I place a detached garage 5 feet from every lot line?

No. The 5-foot rule applies to side and rear lot lines. The front setback follows the principal building's required front setback, and other zoning standards may apply. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638055970231470000))

## What to confirm with the City

- The property's zoning district and whether the lot is conforming
- The allowed garage height, story count, and footprint
- Lot coverage, open space, and driveway access
- Whether a special permit or other zoning relief is required
- Any historic, tree, stormwater, wetlands, or floodplain review
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
