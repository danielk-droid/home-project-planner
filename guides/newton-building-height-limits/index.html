How tall can a house be in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 2 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

In Newton's SR and MR districts, a detached single- or two-family house may be up to 36 feet tall with a sloped roof or 30 feet with a flat roof. The limit is 2.5 stories, or 3 by special permit (Ord. Sec. 3.1.3 and 3.2.3).

Official source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30 ↗

Limits

  • SR1–SR3 detached single-family: 36' sloped roof, 30' flat roof (Sec. 3.1.3). (Zoning Ord. ↗)
  • SR1–SR3: 2.5 stories, or 3 stories by special permit. (Zoning Ord. ↗)
  • MR1–MR4 detached single- or two-family: 36' sloped, 30' flat, 2.5 stories or 3 by special permit (Sec. 3.2.3). (Zoning Ord. ↗)

Measurement

  • Height is measured vertically from the average grade plane to the highest point of the roof, subject to listed exclusions (Sec. 1.5.4.A.1). (Zoning Ord. ↗)
