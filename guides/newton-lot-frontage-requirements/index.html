LOT FRONTAGE

What are the minimum lot frontage and width requirements in Newton zoning?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 2 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

Newton's minimum lot frontage depends on the zoning district and whether the lot was created before or on or after December 7, 1953. For lots created on or after that date, the ordinance lists minimum frontage of 140 feet in SR1, 100 feet in SR2, and 80 feet in SR3. Other districts and older lots have different standards.

Official source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Definitions ↗

Single-residence district frontage

  • For lots created on or after December 7, 1953, Newton's table lists minimum frontage of 140 feet in SR1, 100 feet in SR2, and 80 feet in SR3. (Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Def ↗)

Older lots

  • Newton uses a different dimensional standards table for lots created before December 7, 1953. (Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Def ↗)

Questions and answers

Can I use the SR3 frontage number for every Newton residential lot?
No. The cited table applies to the listed single-residence districts and distinguishes lots by creation date. Confirm the parcel's district and lot history. Source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Definitions ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • The property's current zoning district
  • The legal creation date and frontage measurement of the lot
  • Whether the lot is a corner lot or has unusual street access
  • Whether a nonconforming-lot provision or recorded plan affects the property
  • Whether a proposed subdivision or lot-line change is involved
