# Do I need a permit for scaffolding or sidewalk obstruction in Newton, MA?

> Scaffolding that occupies or obstructs a public sidewalk or right-of-way should be reviewed with Newton Engineering before installation. The available official sources reviewed do not establish the complete application, pedestrian-canopy, insurance, or fee requirements for every scaffolding setup. The property owner or contractor should obtain the City's current direction before blocking public access.

## Public right-of-way review

- Newton Engineering reviews work involving the public right-of-way and related site conditions. (Source: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))

## Separate permit authorities

- Newton ISD states that other City departments have responsibility for certain separate permits and approvals. (Source: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services))

## Questions and answers

### Does a building permit automatically authorize scaffolding on a Newton sidewalk?

The sources reviewed do not establish that. Confirm separate public-way approval with Newton Engineering before placing scaffolding or obstructing a sidewalk. (Source: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the scaffold is on private property or the public right-of-way
- The current sidewalk or public-way obstruction application
- Whether a pedestrian canopy or alternate walking route is required
- Required barricades, reflective markers, insurance, or bonds
- The approved occupancy period and inspection process
- Contact: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering)
