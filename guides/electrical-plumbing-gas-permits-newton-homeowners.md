# Can a homeowner do electrical, plumbing or gas work in Newton, MA?

> Newton's electrical and plumbing/gas permit submissions require an appropriately signed license. Massachusetts issues plumbing permits only to licensed plumbers. The state lists minor repairs, such as fixing a leaky faucet or clearing a blocked drain, as not needing a permit.

## Newton requirements

- Electrical permit submissions require an appropriately signed electrical license. (Source: [Electrical Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/electrical-permits))
- Electrical submissions need liability insurance and workers' compensation unless the applicant is a sole proprietor. (Source: [Electrical Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/electrical-permits))
- Plumbing and gas submissions require an appropriately signed plumbing or gas license. (Source: [Plumbing & Gas Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/plumbing-gas-permits))
- Replacing a gas water heater requires both a plumbing permit and a gas permit. (Source: [Plumbing & Gas Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/plumbing-gas-permits))

## Massachusetts rules

- Massachusetts issues plumbing permits only to licensed plumbers. (Source: [Plumbers and Gas Fitters Consumer Fact Sheet (Mass.gov)](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/plumbers-and-gas-fitters-consumer-fact-sheet))
- Only licensed professionals may install, remove or repair plumbing. (Source: [Plumbers and Gas Fitters Consumer Fact Sheet (Mass.gov)](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/plumbers-and-gas-fitters-consumer-fact-sheet))
- Fixing a leaky faucet, valve or other working part, or clearing a blocked drain, is a minor repair that does not need a permit. (Source: [Plumbers and Gas Fitters Consumer Fact Sheet (Mass.gov)](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/plumbers-and-gas-fitters-consumer-fact-sheet))
- Licensed master or journeyman plumbers may do gas fitting, but a gas-fitter license does not cover plumbing work. (Source: [Plumbers and Gas Fitters Consumer Fact Sheet (Mass.gov)](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/plumbers-and-gas-fitters-consumer-fact-sheet))

## Questions and answers

### Can I pull my own plumbing permit in Massachusetts?

The state says plumbing permits are issued only to licensed plumbers. (Source: [Plumbers and Gas Fitters Consumer Fact Sheet (Mass.gov)](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/plumbers-and-gas-fitters-consumer-fact-sheet))

### Do I need a permit to fix a leaky faucet?

No. The state lists fixing a leaky faucet, valve or other working part, or clearing a blocked drain, as minor repairs that need no permit. (Source: [Plumbers and Gas Fitters Consumer Fact Sheet (Mass.gov)](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/plumbers-and-gas-fitters-consumer-fact-sheet))

### What permits does a gas water heater replacement need in Newton?

Both a plumbing permit and a gas permit. (Source: [Plumbing & Gas Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/plumbing-gas-permits))

### What does a Newton electrical permit application need?

An appropriately signed electrical license, plus liability insurance and workers' compensation unless the applicant is a sole proprietor. (Source: [Electrical Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/electrical-permits))

### Can a gas fitter do plumbing?

No. The state says a gas-fitter license does not authorize plumbing work. (Source: [Plumbers and Gas Fitters Consumer Fact Sheet (Mass.gov)](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/plumbers-and-gas-fitters-consumer-fact-sheet))
