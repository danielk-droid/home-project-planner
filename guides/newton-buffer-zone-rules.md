# What work is restricted within the 100-foot wetland buffer zone in Newton, MA?

> Newton requires Conservation Commission review for land-altering work within 100 feet of a vegetated wetland, intermittent stream, or pond. The City also identifies a 200-foot review area near perennial streams. The exact restrictions depend on the resource area, proposed activity, and applicable exemptions.

## The 100-foot area

- Newton identifies the 100-foot area near vegetated wetlands, intermittent streams, and ponds as an area where land-altering work must be reviewed and permitted by the Conservation Commission. (Source: [Newton Wetlands Permitting Information](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/conservation-office/wetlands-permitting-rev))

## Activities that may be regulated

- Newton lists grading, construction, tree or shrub removal, and drainage projects among land-altering activities subject to review. (Source: [Newton Wetlands Permitting Information](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/conservation-office/wetlands-permitting-rev))

## Questions and answers

### Can I grade or install drainage within the 100-foot area without checking?

No. Newton expressly lists grading and drainage projects among activities that may require Conservation Commission review. (Source: [Newton Wetlands Permitting Information](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/conservation-office/wetlands-permitting-rev))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the property is within the 100-foot or 200-foot review area
- The exact resource-area boundary and whether a survey is needed
- Whether the work is exempt or requires an RDA or NOI
- Whether a City floodplain or stormwater review is also required
- Whether tree-removal or construction permits are required separately
- Contact: [Newton Conservation Commission](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/conservation-office)
