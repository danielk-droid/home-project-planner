# What legal hardship standard is required to get a zoning variance in Newton, MA?

> Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 40A, Section 10 sets a strict variance test. The authority must find special circumstances involving the soil conditions, shape, or topography of the land or structure; substantial hardship from literal enforcement; and that relief can be granted without substantial detriment to the public good or substantial derogation from zoning's purpose. A variance is not simply permission to avoid an inconvenient rule.

## Special land or structure conditions

- The statute requires circumstances relating to soil conditions, shape, or topography that particularly affect the land or structure and do not generally affect the zoning district. (Source: [Massachusetts General Laws, Chapter 40A, Section 10, Variances](https://malegislature.gov/laws/generallaws/parti/titlevii/chapter40a/section10))

## Hardship and public-good findings

- The authority must find substantial hardship from literal enforcement and determine that relief can be granted without substantial detriment to the public good or substantial derogation from the ordinance's purpose. (Source: [Massachusetts General Laws, Chapter 40A, Section 10, Variances](https://malegislature.gov/laws/generallaws/parti/titlevii/chapter40a/section10))

## Questions and answers

### Is financial cost alone enough to prove a zoning variance hardship?

The statute requires the specified land or structure circumstances as well as substantial hardship and the public-good findings. Financial cost alone is not the complete statutory test. (Source: [Massachusetts General Laws, Chapter 40A, Section 10, Variances](https://malegislature.gov/laws/generallaws/parti/titlevii/chapter40a/section10))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the requested relief is legally eligible for a variance
- Which soil, shape, or topography conditions affect the property
- Whether the hardship is specific to the property rather than the zoning district generally
- Whether a special permit or by-right design is an alternative
- The current Newton application, hearing, and filing requirements
