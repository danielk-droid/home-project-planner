FENCES

Do I need a permit for a fence in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 3 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

Yes, a Newton fence permit is required for a perimeter fence greater than 4 feet tall. The state building code separately exempts fences not over 7 feet from its building-permit requirement, but that exemption does not remove Newton's local fence rules.

Official source: Newton Revised Ordinances, Chapter 5, Fence Regulations ↗ Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2 ↗

Newton's local fence permit

  • Newton requires a fence permit before erecting a perimeter fence greater than 4 feet tall. (Newton Revised Ordinances, Chapter 5, Fe ↗)
  • For a fence bordering or parallel to a front lot line, the general height limit is 4 feet. A taller fence may be allowed with additional setbacks, subject to the ordinance's maximum height and openness rules. (Newton Revised Ordinances, Chapter 5, Fe ↗)

State building-code exemption

  • The tenth-edition Massachusetts building code exempts fences not over 7 feet from the building permit required under that code. This is separate from Newton's local fence permit. (Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 C ↗)

Questions and answers

Does a fence under 7 feet automatically avoid all permits in Newton?
No. Newton's separate fence ordinance requires a local fence permit for a perimeter fence greater than 4 feet tall. Source: Newton Revised Ordinances, Chapter 5, Fence Regulations ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Whether the property is in a Local Historic District, is a Local Landmark, or has a Preservation Restriction
  • The exact fence height and location allowed on a corner lot
  • Whether wetlands, easements, or other site restrictions affect the proposed fence
  • Whether the fence is a perimeter fence or a different type covered by a separate rule

Contact: Inspectional Services Department ↗
