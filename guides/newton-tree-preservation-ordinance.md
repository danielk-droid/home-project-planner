# Do I need a permit to cut down a tree in Newton, MA?

> Often, yes. Trees 6 inches or more in diameter at breast height (measured 4½ feet above grade) on covered land are protected. You need a tree permit before removing a protected tree or starting work under an Exterior Work Permit. Even exempt trees need an exemption permit before removal.

## What the ordinance says

- Protected trees include trees at least 6 inches DBH on covered land, plus replacement trees (Sec. 21-81). (Source: [Tree Preservation Ordinance text (Ch. 21, Art. III)](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/117204/638445534507730000))
- DBH is measured 4½ feet above grade at the base of the tree (Sec. 21-81). (Source: [Tree Preservation Ordinance text (Ch. 21, Art. III)](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/117204/638445534507730000))
- A tree permit is required before removing a protected, non-exempt tree, or before starting work under an Exterior Work Permit (Sec. 21-82(b)). (Source: [Tree Preservation Ordinance text (Ch. 21, Art. III)](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/117204/638445534507730000))
- Even an exempt tree requires an exemption permit before removal (Sec. 21-82(c)). (Source: [Tree Preservation Ordinance text (Ch. 21, Art. III)](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/117204/638445534507730000))

## Replacement

- Required replacement is due within 18 months of the tree permit, or before the property changes hands, whichever comes first (Sec. 21-85(a)). (Source: [Tree Preservation Ordinance text (Ch. 21, Art. III)](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/117204/638445534507730000))
- Replacement trees must be planted on the same lot (Sec. 21-85(a)(2)). (Source: [Tree Preservation Ordinance text (Ch. 21, Art. III)](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/117204/638445534507730000))
- Applicants may plant replacements, pay in lieu, or combine the two. (Source: [Tree Preservation Ordinance](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/parks-recreation-culture/urban-forestry/tree-preservation-ordinance))
- Replacement trees are protected and get at least Significant Tree status, whatever their trunk size (Sec. 21-85(a)(5)). (Source: [Tree Preservation Ordinance text (Ch. 21, Art. III)](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/117204/638445534507730000))

## Questions and answers

### What size tree is protected in Newton?

Trees at least 6 inches DBH on covered land, measured 4½ feet above grade. (Source: [Tree Preservation Ordinance text (Ch. 21, Art. III)](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/117204/638445534507730000))

### Do I need a tree permit for a construction project?

A tree permit is required before starting work under an Exterior Work Permit (Sec. 21-82(b)). (Source: [Tree Preservation Ordinance text (Ch. 21, Art. III)](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/117204/638445534507730000))

### Can I pay instead of replanting?

Yes. The City lists planting, payment in lieu, or a combination. (Source: [Tree Preservation Ordinance](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/parks-recreation-culture/urban-forestry/tree-preservation-ordinance))

### When must replacement trees be planted?

Within 18 months of the tree permit, or before the property changes hands, whichever comes first. (Source: [Tree Preservation Ordinance text (Ch. 21, Art. III)](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/117204/638445534507730000))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether your land is 'covered land' under the ordinance.
- Replacement amounts from the City's current table.
