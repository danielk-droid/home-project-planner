HISTORIC LANDMARKS

How do landmark designation rules work for historic homes in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 3 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

A Newton City Landmark requires historic review before exterior work begins. A property with a Preservation Restriction also requires review under the terms of that restriction. The required approval and design standards depend on the individual property's designation and the scope of the proposed work.

Official source: Newton Historical Commission ↗ Newton Historic Preservation, Submit an Application ↗

Local Landmark review

  • Newton requires a Local Landmark Review Application for changes to a designated City Landmark, with Historical Commission approval before exterior work begins. (Historical Commission ↗)

Preservation Restrictions

  • Newton says the terms of each Preservation Restriction specify what work may or may not be allowed and require review before exterior work. (Historical Commission ↗)

Questions and answers

Does landmark approval replace a building permit?
No. Historic approval and building permits are separate approvals. Newton says a historic certificate is necessary but not sufficient for a building permit. Source: Newton Historic Preservation Certificate Review Process ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Whether the property is a City Landmark or has a Preservation Restriction
  • Which review application applies
  • Whether staff review or a full Commission hearing is required
  • Whether the proposed work meets the property's design guidelines
  • Whether other City permits are also required

Contact: Newton Historical Commission ↗
