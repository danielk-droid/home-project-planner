CORNER LOTS

How do setbacks work on a corner lot in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 2 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

A corner lot can have different setback consequences from an interior lot because it borders two streets. Newton's zoning ordinance contains a specific definition of a corner lot and separate dimensional standards for lot boundaries. Do not assume one street-facing side is automatically treated as a side yard; confirm the parcel's zoning interpretation with Planning.

Official source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3 ↗

Corner-lot classification

  • Newton's zoning ordinance defines a corner lot separately from an interior lot and uses that definition when applying zoning requirements. (Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Sec ↗)

Property-specific setback review

  • The applicable front, side, and rear setbacks must be determined from the parcel's zoning district and the ordinance provisions that apply to its lot boundaries. (Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Sec ↗)

Questions and answers

Does every corner lot have two front setbacks?
The exact classification and setback treatment should be confirmed from the current zoning ordinance and parcel map. The sources reviewed do not support one universal answer for every corner lot. Source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3 ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Which lot lines the City treats as front, side, and rear
  • The lot's zoning district and dimensional table
  • Any corner visibility or sight-triangle restrictions
  • Whether an existing structure is nonconforming
  • The setback requirements for the proposed building or site improvement

Contact: Planning & Development Department ↗
