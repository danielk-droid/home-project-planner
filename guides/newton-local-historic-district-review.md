# What needs approval in a Newton local historic district?

> In a Newton local historic district, exterior changes, including hardscaping, need review by the Historic District Commission. Visible alterations need a Certificate of Appropriateness. In-kind work, changes that can't be seen, and excluded work can get a Certificate of Non-Applicability, and in-kind repairs may qualify for administrative approval.

## What the City says

- Exterior changes in a local historic district, including hardscaping, require HDC review. (Source: [Historic Preservation](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/historic-preservation))
- A Certificate of Appropriateness covers visible alterations the commission finds appropriate. (Source: [Auburndale Historic District Commission](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/historic-preservation/local-historic-district-commissions/auburndale-hdc))
- A Certificate of Non-Applicability covers in-kind work, changes that can't be seen, or excluded work. (Source: [Auburndale Historic District Commission](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/historic-preservation/local-historic-district-commissions/auburndale-hdc))
- Exterior building changes require an application, and in-kind repairs may qualify for administrative approval. (Source: [Auburndale Historic District Commission](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/historic-preservation/local-historic-district-commissions/auburndale-hdc))

## Questions and answers

### Do I need approval to replace windows in a historic district?

Exterior building changes need an application. In-kind work may qualify for a Certificate of Non-Applicability or administrative approval. (Source: [Auburndale Historic District Commission](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/historic-preservation/local-historic-district-commissions/auburndale-hdc))

### Does a patio or walkway need review?

The City says exterior changes, including hardscaping, need HDC review. (Source: [Historic Preservation](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/historic-preservation))

### What is a Certificate of Appropriateness?

Approval for visible alterations that the commission finds appropriate. (Source: [Auburndale Historic District Commission](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/historic-preservation/local-historic-district-commissions/auburndale-hdc))
