# Can you convert an existing garage into an ADU in Newton, MA?

> Newton allows certain existing garages and other accessory structures to be converted into detached ADUs, subject to the City's size, zoning, and building-code requirements. A qualifying existing structure may be eligible for a larger by-right ADU under Newton's four-year condition. The conversion still needs the required building and safety approvals.

## Existing garage eligibility

- Newton's ADU guidance identifies an existing structure, including a garage, as a possible location for a detached ADU and describes conditions for the 1,200-square-foot by-right path. (Source: [Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/zoning-and-development/zoning-updates/accessory-dwelling-units-adu))

## Building-code approval

- The state ADU regulation does not supersede the Massachusetts Building Code or Fire Code. (Source: [760 CMR 71.00 — Protected Use ADUs](https://www.mass.gov/doc/760-cmr-71-protected-use-accessory-dwelling-units/download))

## Questions and answers

### Does an existing garage automatically qualify for a 1,200-square-foot ADU?

No. Newton lists conditions involving the existing structure and its approval history. The property's specific situation must be checked against the current ordinance. (Source: [Newton Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/zoning-and-development/zoning-updates/accessory-dwelling-units-adu))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the garage was lawfully established and meets Newton's four-year condition
- Whether the conversion meets the ADU size and gross-floor-area limits
- Whether the existing setbacks and height comply or have nonconforming status
- Required foundation, insulation, vapor barrier, and fire-separation work
- Utility connection and separate service requirements
- Historic review if the structure may be historically significant
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
