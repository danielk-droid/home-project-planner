# When is an on-site drywell or stormwater infiltration system required in Newton, MA?

> Newton's stormwater ordinance regulates certain land-disturbance and new-impervious-area projects, but the sources reviewed do not establish one universal drywell size or a citywide rule requiring every homeowner to install a drywell. Whether an infiltration system is needed depends on the project, site conditions, and Newton's stormwater requirements.

## Stormwater permit triggers

- Newton's stormwater ordinance identifies land disturbance over 5,000 square feet and certain residential projects creating 401 to 1,000 square feet of new impervious area as permit categories. (Source: [Newton Stormwater Management and Erosion Control Ordinance](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/104834/638260676720970000))

## Engineering review

- Newton Engineering reviews site plans involving new drainage and expansions of impervious area. (Source: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))

## Questions and answers

### Is a drywell always required for a new roof or driveway?

The official sources reviewed do not establish that universal rule. Ask Engineering whether the project requires infiltration, a stormwater plan, or another approved drainage approach. (Source: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the project triggers Newton's stormwater permit thresholds
- The required design storm and runoff volume used for sizing
- Whether soil testing or groundwater separation information is required
- The drywell location, overflow route, and maintenance requirements
- Whether wetland, floodplain, or utility constraints affect infiltration
- Contact: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering)
