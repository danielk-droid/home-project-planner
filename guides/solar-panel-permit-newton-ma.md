# Do I need a permit for solar panels in Newton, MA?

> Yes. Newton's permit fee schedule lists a building permit category for solar panels without structural work, a separate category for solar panels with structural work, and an electrical permit category for solar.

## What the City lists

- The fee schedule lists a building-permit category for solar panels without structural work. (Source: [Permit Fee Schedule](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29405/639029478067930000))
- It separately lists structural-work building permits for solar panels. (Source: [Permit Fee Schedule](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29405/639029478067930000))
- It lists an electrical permit category for solar panels. (Source: [Permit Fee Schedule](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29405/639029478067930000))
- Electrical permit applications are filed online and require an appropriately signed electrical license. (Source: [Electrical Permits](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services/electrical-permits))

## Questions and answers

### Does a solar installation need an electrical permit in Newton?

The fee schedule lists an electrical permit category for solar panels. (Source: [Permit Fee Schedule](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29405/639029478067930000))

### Is a building permit needed if there's no structural work?

The fee schedule lists a building permit category for solar panels without structural work. (Source: [Permit Fee Schedule](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29405/639029478067930000))

### Do historic district rules apply to solar?

Exterior changes in local historic districts require HDC review. (Source: [Historic Preservation](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/historic-preservation))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether your roof needs structural work.
- Current fees for each category.
