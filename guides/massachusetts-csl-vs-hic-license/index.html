What is the difference between a Massachusetts CSL and HIC license?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 3 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

A Massachusetts Construction Supervisor License (CSL) concerns supervision of construction work regulated by the building code. Home Improvement Contractor (HIC) registration is a separate consumer-protection registration for contractors doing covered home-improvement work. A project may involve both requirements, so homeowners should not treat one credential as a substitute for the other.

Official source: Massachusetts When Is a Construction Supervisor License Required? ↗ Massachusetts HIC Contractor Resources ↗

Construction Supervisor License

  • Massachusetts says certain construction projects require oversight by a licensed construction supervisor or registered design professional. (Massachusetts When Is a Construction Sup ↗)

Home Improvement Contractor registration

  • Massachusetts HIC rules separately regulate contractor registration, written contracts, permits, payments, and access to the Guaranty Fund. (Massachusetts HIC Contractor Resources ↗)

Questions and answers

Does an HIC registration replace a CSL?
No. The CSL and HIC programs address different requirements: construction supervision under the building code and contractor registration under the home-improvement law. Source: Massachusetts When Is a Construction Supervisor License Required? ↗, Massachusetts HIC Contractor Resources ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Whether the project requires a CSL or registered design professional
  • Whether the contractor must hold active HIC registration
  • Whether the contractor's registration and license match the work being performed
  • Whether the homeowner is relying on an applicable owner exemption
  • Whether the proposed contract meets Massachusetts home-improvement contract rules
