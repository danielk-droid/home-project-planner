# Do I need a permit to repave or widen a driveway in Newton, MA?

> A driveway project may need City review when it changes a curb cut, site grading, or the amount of impervious surface. Newton Engineering specifically reviews driveway expansions and curb-cut modifications. A simple repaving project should be checked against the property's site conditions and any historic restrictions.

## Engineering review

- Newton Engineering reviews site plans involving expansions of impervious area, including driveways, and modifications to curb cuts. (Source: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))
- The state building-code driveway exemption applies only to qualifying driveways that are not more than 30 inches above adjacent grade, are not over a basement or lower story, and are not part of an accessible route. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## Historic properties

- Exterior driveway changes at properties in Newton's Local Historic Districts require a Historic District Application. (Source: [Newton Historic Preservation, Submit an Application](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/historic-preservation/submit-an-application))

## Questions and answers

### Does Newton publish one maximum curb-cut width for every residential driveway?

The sources reviewed for this guide do not establish one universal residential curb-cut width. Confirm the proposed opening width and location with Newton Engineering. (Source: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the work changes the curb cut or public right-of-way
- The allowed curb-cut width and spacing for the particular property
- Whether the work increases impervious area or changes grades
- Whether the lot has historic, wetlands, floodplain, or drainage restrictions
- Whether the proposed parking layout complies with Newton zoning
- Contact: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering)
