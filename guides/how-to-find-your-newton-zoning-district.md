# How do I find my zoning district in Newton, MA?

> Look up your address on the City's interactive Zoning map or the Newton GIS Browser, both linked from the City GIS page. Under the zoning ordinance, the GIS zoning districts make up Newton's Official Zoning Map.

## Official tools

- The City GIS page links to an interactive Zoning map and to the Newton GIS Browser for layers and property information. (Source: [City of Newton GIS](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/information-technology/gis))
- GIS zoning districts constitute Newton's Official Zoning Map (Sec. 1.3.2.C). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))
- Printed copies of the Official Zoning Map are kept by the City Clerk (Sec. 1.3.2.E). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

## Questions and answers

### Where is the Newton zoning map?

The City GIS page links to an interactive Zoning map and the Newton GIS Browser. (Source: [City of Newton GIS](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/information-technology/gis))

### Is the GIS map the official zoning map?

Yes. Sec. 1.3.2.C of the ordinance says the GIS zoning districts constitute the Official Zoning Map. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### Can I get a printed zoning map?

The ordinance says the City Clerk keeps printed copies (Sec. 1.3.2.E). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

### What do the district names mean?

SR1–SR3 are Single Residence districts and MR1–MR4 are Multi-Residence districts (Sec. 1.3.1). (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638717834291930000))

## What to confirm with the City

- Your district if the property sits on a district boundary.
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
