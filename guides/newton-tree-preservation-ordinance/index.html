TREES

Do I need a permit to cut down a tree in Newton, MA?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 2 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

Often, yes. Trees 6 inches or more in diameter at breast height (measured 4½ feet above grade) on covered land are protected. You need a tree permit before removing a protected tree or starting work under an Exterior Work Permit. Even exempt trees need an exemption permit before removal.

Official source: Tree Preservation Ordinance text (Ch. 21, Art. III) ↗ Tree Preservation Ordinance ↗

What the ordinance says

  • Protected trees include trees at least 6 inches DBH on covered land, plus replacement trees (Sec. 21-81). (Ord. text ↗)
  • DBH is measured 4½ feet above grade at the base of the tree (Sec. 21-81). (Ord. text ↗)
  • A tree permit is required before removing a protected, non-exempt tree, or before starting work under an Exterior Work Permit (Sec. 21-82(b)). (Ord. text ↗)
  • Even an exempt tree requires an exemption permit before removal (Sec. 21-82(c)). (Ord. text ↗)

Replacement

  • Required replacement is due within 18 months of the tree permit, or before the property changes hands, whichever comes first (Sec. 21-85(a)). (Ord. text ↗)
  • Replacement trees must be planted on the same lot (Sec. 21-85(a)(2)). (Ord. text ↗)
  • Applicants may plant replacements, pay in lieu, or combine the two. (Tree ordinance page ↗)
  • Replacement trees are protected and get at least Significant Tree status, whatever their trunk size (Sec. 21-85(a)(5)). (Ord. text ↗)

Questions and answers

What size tree is protected in Newton?
Trees at least 6 inches DBH on covered land, measured 4½ feet above grade. Source: Tree Preservation Ordinance text (Ch. 21, Art. III) ↗
Do I need a tree permit for a construction project?
A tree permit is required before starting work under an Exterior Work Permit (Sec. 21-82(b)). Source: Tree Preservation Ordinance text (Ch. 21, Art. III) ↗
Can I pay instead of replanting?
Yes. The City lists planting, payment in lieu, or a combination. Source: Tree Preservation Ordinance ↗
When must replacement trees be planted?
Within 18 months of the tree permit, or before the property changes hands, whichever comes first. Source: Tree Preservation Ordinance text (Ch. 21, Art. III) ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Whether your land is 'covered land' under the ordinance.
  • Replacement amounts from the City's current table.
