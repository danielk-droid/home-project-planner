# How many parking spaces are required for single- and two-family homes in Newton, MA?

> Newton's current parking table lists 2 parking stalls per unit for a single-family or two-family dwelling. Separate zoning rules control where parking stalls may be placed, including limits within required street and side-yard setbacks. Confirm the rules for the property's exact zoning district and site layout.

## Minimum parking count

- Newton's parking table lists 2 parking stalls per unit for a single-family or two-family dwelling. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 5.1](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/639035614023930000))

## Parking location

- Newton's zoning ordinance separately regulates parking stalls within required street and side-lot-line setbacks. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638055970231470000))

## Questions and answers

### Does the 2-per-unit rule mean all spaces can be placed in the front yard?

No. The required parking count and the rules governing where spaces may be placed are separate zoning questions. (Source: [Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Section 3.4.3](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/29823/638055970231470000))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the property is single-family, two-family, or another residential use under zoning
- Whether tandem parking is allowed for the proposed layout
- The applicable front- and side-setback parking exceptions
- Whether the property is within an overlay district or subject to a special permit
- Whether ADU parking rules change the result
- Contact: [Planning & Development Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning)
