# How do I apply for a tree removal permit in Newton, MA?

> Newton requires a permit to remove private-property trees with a trunk diameter of 6 inches or greater. The City also says a tree permit is required when construction takes place, whether or not trees are being removed. The application type depends on the property, tree, and project circumstances.

## Tree size threshold

- Newton states that, as of March 1, 2024, a permit is required to remove all trees with a trunk diameter of 6 inches or greater. (Source: [Tree Preservation Ordinance](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/parks-recreation-culture/urban-forestry/tree-preservation-ordinance))

## Construction permits

- Newton's tree-preservation guidance says a tree permit is also required when construction takes place, whether or not trees are involved. (Source: [Tree Preservation Ordinance](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/parks-recreation-culture/urban-forestry/tree-preservation-ordinance))

## Questions and answers

### Is the current protected-tree threshold 8 inches?

No. Newton's current tree-preservation guidance states that the permit threshold is 6 inches in trunk diameter or greater. (Source: [Tree Preservation Ordinance](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/parks-recreation-culture/urban-forestry/tree-preservation-ordinance))

## What to confirm with the City

- Which tree permit type applies to the property and project
- How the City measures the tree trunk diameter
- Whether the tree is exempt because of the property or project category
- Whether construction requires a permit even if no tree will be removed
- Required replacement planting or payment conditions
- Contact: [Newton Urban Forestry / Tree Warden](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/parks-recreation-culture/urban-forestry)
