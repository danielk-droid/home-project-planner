# When do you need to file an RDA or NOI with the Newton Conservation Commission?

> Newton uses a Request for Determination of Applicability (RDA) and a Notice of Intent (NOI) as separate wetlands filing paths. The City lists a $50 filing fee for each application type. An NOI requires additional materials, including scaled plans, a project narrative, performance-standard information, and abutter-notification documents.

## RDA application

- Newton lists an RDA Process Instructions and Checklist, WPA Form 1, scaled plans, and a $50 City filing fee as RDA application materials. (Source: [Newton Wetlands Permitting Information](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/conservation-office/wetlands-permitting-rev))

## NOI application

- Newton lists WPA Form 3, scaled plans, a project narrative, performance-standard information, a $50 filing fee, and abutter-notification documents among NOI application materials. (Source: [Newton Wetlands Permitting Information](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/conservation-office/wetlands-permitting-rev))

## Questions and answers

### Does an RDA require the same submission package as an NOI?

No. Newton lists different filing materials for the two application types. The City should confirm which filing applies to the proposed activity. (Source: [Newton Wetlands Permitting Information](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/conservation-office/wetlands-permitting-rev))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the project requires an RDA, NOI, or another determination
- Whether the activity is within a regulated resource area
- The applicable filing fee at the time of submission
- The plans, narrative, and technical attachments needed
- The current abutter list and proof-of-service requirements
- Whether separate building, Engineering, tree, or historic permits are required
- Contact: [Newton Conservation Commission](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/conservation-office)
