# When can a Newton homeowner pull their own building permit?

> Massachusetts building-code rules provide a homeowner exemption from certain construction-supervisor licensing requirements when the homeowner performs the work and supervises hired workers. This is not a general exemption from building permits or other safety requirements. Newton homeowners should confirm permit eligibility and the HIC Guaranty Fund consequences before applying in their own name.

## Homeowner licensing exemption

- The Massachusetts construction-supervisor licensing regulation contains an exemption for a homeowner performing work for which a building permit is required, subject to the stated supervision conditions. (Source: [780 CMR Tenth Edition, Chapter 110.R5, Licensing of Construction Supervisors](https://www.mass.gov/regulations/780-CMR-tenth-edition-chapter-110r5-licensing-of-construction-supervisors))

## Permit and consumer-protection consequences

- Massachusetts says a homeowner who applies for the building permit may not be eligible for compensation from the HIC Guaranty Fund. (Source: [Massachusetts HIC Homeowner Resources](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/hic-homeowner-resources))

## Questions and answers

### Does the homeowner exemption mean I can do regulated work without a building permit?

No. The exemption concerns construction-supervisor licensing; it does not eliminate the underlying building permit or applicable code requirements. (Source: [780 CMR Tenth Edition, Chapter 110.R5, Licensing of Construction Supervisors](https://www.mass.gov/regulations/780-CMR-tenth-edition-chapter-110r5-licensing-of-construction-supervisors); [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the homeowner meets the exact owner-exemption requirements
- Whether the building is a one- or two-family dwelling or another building type
- Whether any work must be supervised by a CSL or registered design professional
- Whether the homeowner's permit application affects HIC Guaranty Fund eligibility
- Newton's current application requirements and permit-holder responsibilities
- Contact: [Inspectional Services Department](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/inspectional-services)
