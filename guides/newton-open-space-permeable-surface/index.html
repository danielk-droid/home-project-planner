OPEN SPACE

What are Newton's minimum open space and permeable surface requirements?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 3 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

Newton's required open space and maximum lot coverage vary by zoning district and lot creation date. For single-residence lots created on or after December 7, 1953, the ordinance lists minimum open space of 70% in SR1, 65% in SR2, and 50% in SR3. Older lots have a different table, so a single citywide percentage is not correct.

Official source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Definitions ↗

Standards for newer single-residence lots

  • For lots created on or after December 7, 1953, Newton's table lists minimum open space of 70% in SR1, 65% in SR2, and 50% in SR3. (Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Def ↗)

Older single-residence lots

  • Newton uses a separate dimensional table for lots created before December 7, 1953. (Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Def ↗)
  • Newton Engineering reviews expansions of impervious area, including patios, pools, and driveways. (Newton Engineering Division ↗)

Questions and answers

Can I use the SR2 open-space percentage for every Newton property?
No. The applicable percentage depends on the zoning district and the lot's creation date. Other districts have their own standards. Source: Newton Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 30, Definitions ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • The property's zoning district and legal lot creation date
  • How Newton classifies each existing and proposed surface
  • The lot's current lot coverage and open-space calculations
  • Whether a stormwater permit is required
  • Whether the property is in a VCOD or another overlay district

Contact: Planning & Development Department ↗
