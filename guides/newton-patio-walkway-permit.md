# Do I need a permit for a patio or walkway in Newton, MA?

> A patio or walkway is not automatically covered by the state building-code exemption for sidewalks and driveways. Newton Engineering reviews expansions of impervious surface, including patios, and grade alterations. Properties in a Local Historic District also need historic review for exterior hardscaping changes.

## Engineering and drainage review

- Newton Engineering reviews site plans for expansions of impervious area, including patios, and for grade alterations. (Source: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering))
- Newton's stormwater ordinance identifies certain new impervious-area projects and land-disturbance projects as requiring stormwater review. (Source: [Newton Stormwater Management and Erosion Control Ordinance](https://www.newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/104834/638260676720970000))

## Historic district review

- Newton requires a Historic District Application for exterior changes, including walkways and hardscaping, at properties in its Local Historic Districts. (Source: [Newton Historic Preservation, Submit an Application](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/planning/divisions/historic-preservation/submit-an-application))

## Questions and answers

### Does the state driveway exemption automatically cover a patio?

No. The cited state exemption names sidewalks and driveways, not patios. Confirm the permit requirements for the proposed patio with Newton. (Source: [Massachusetts State Building Code, 780 CMR, Tenth Edition, Section 105.2](https://www.mass.gov/doc/bbrs-10th-edition-building-code/download))

## What to confirm with the City

- Whether the proposed surface is counted as impervious area under Newton's rules
- Whether the project crosses a stormwater permit threshold
- Whether grading or drainage changes require Engineering review
- Whether a building permit is required for the exact design
- The property's applicable lot-coverage and open-space limits
- Contact: [Newton Engineering Division](https://www.newtonma.gov/government/public-works/engineering)
