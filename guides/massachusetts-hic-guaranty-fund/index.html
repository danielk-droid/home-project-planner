How does the Massachusetts HIC arbitration and Guaranty Fund protect homeowners?

Updated November 13, 2026 · Built from 4 official City of Newton and Massachusetts sources, each linked below.

SHORT ANSWER

Massachusetts provides an HIC arbitration and Guaranty Fund process for eligible homeowners who have a qualifying dispute with a registered home-improvement contractor. The Guaranty Fund recovery is capped at $10,000 for actual loss. State law also requires covered home-improvement contracts over $1,000 to be in writing.

Official source: Massachusetts HIC Arbitration and Guaranty Fund Regulation ↗ Massachusetts HIC Homeowner Resources ↗ Massachusetts Home Improvement Contract Sample Language ↗

Guaranty Fund limit

  • Massachusetts regulations state that an eligible claimant may recover once up to, and no more than, $10,000 from the Guaranty Fund for actual loss. (Massachusetts HIC Arbitration and Guaran ↗)

Written contracts

  • Massachusetts requires home-improvement contracts over $1,000 to be in writing. (Massachusetts Home Improvement Contract ↗)

Questions and answers

Can every homeowner recover $10,000 from the HIC Guaranty Fund?
No. The fund has eligibility requirements, and the $10,000 figure is a maximum recovery for eligible claims, not an automatic payment. Source: Massachusetts HIC Arbitration and Guaranty Fund Regulation ↗

What to confirm with the City

  • Whether the contractor was properly registered when the work was contracted
  • Whether the homeowner and property meet the Guaranty Fund eligibility conditions
  • Whether arbitration is required or appropriate before a fund claim
  • Whether the homeowner pulled the building permit instead of the contractor
  • Current filing deadlines and claim documentation

Contact: Massachusetts Office of Consumer Affairs and Business Regulation ↗
